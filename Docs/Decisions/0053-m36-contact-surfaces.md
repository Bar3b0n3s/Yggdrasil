## Context

The facts this record relies on, each checked in the code at `7bfd889d`:

1. **Friction and restitution are per body.** `RigidBodyComponent::Friction` and `Restitution` become `BodyDescription::Friction` and `Restitution` (`Scene/PhysicsSystem.cpp`, `MakeBodySettings`), Jolt's `BodyCreationSettings::mFriction` and `mRestitution` (`Physics/PhysicsWorld.cpp`, `CreateBody`); an implicit static body takes its own `TerrainCollider`'s values or `BodyDescription`'s defaults (0.5, 0).
2. **Jolt's combination is the default.** Nothing calls `SetCombineFriction` or `SetCombineRestitution`, so `ContactConstraintManager`'s default functions apply: `Sqrt(f1 * f2)` and `max(r1, r2)` (`Vendor/JoltPhysics/Jolt/Physics/Constraints/ContactConstraintManager.h`). Jolt fills `ContactSettings` (`mCombinedFriction`, `mCombinedRestitution`, `mRelativeLinearSurfaceVelocity`) with those values before every `OnContactAdded` and `OnContactPersisted`: for a new manifold, a manifold reused from the cache (`AddConstraintsFromCachedManifold`'s loop, the settings recomputed for each) and in the continuous-collision pass (`OnCCDContactAdded`, which never applies the surface velocity). The friction constraint applies the relative surface velocity along the contact's tangents (`CalculateFrictionConstraintProperties`).
3. **The listener ignored the settings.** `PhysicsContactListener::OnContactAdded` took `ContactSettings& /*settings*/`, and `OnContactPersisted` returned at once unless a speculative contact was pending. The listener holds the world's `PhysicsBodyRecord`s and resolves a sub-shape to its collider index with `ResolvePhysicsCollider` (`Physics/Private/PhysicsContactListener.cpp`); the records are written on the main thread between steps and read by Jolt's workers during them.
4. **Each collider has its identity.** A collider's index (`ColliderShapeDescription::UserData`) is its compound's sub-shape user data; `PhysicsBodyEntry::Plan.Colliders[index]` is its entity, and `GatheredColliders` names the body a non-owner collider entity was gathered into (`Scene/Private/PhysicsSystemState.h`).
5. **A physics component's signal recomposes the scene.** `PhysicsSystem::State::OnComponentChanged` sets `ComponentsChanged`, and the next PreStep runs `Rebuild`, which composes the whole scene (`ComposePhysicsBodies`).
6. **Waking.** `Utils::WakeBodiesIn` wakes the bodies inside a box (Jolt wakes no neighbour itself), and `PhysicsWorld::GetSubShapeBounds` gives a collider's world box.
7. **The character's ground.** Jolt's update stores the ground's body and sub-shape (`CharacterBase::GetGroundBodyID`, `GetGroundSubShapeID`), its velocity (`GetGroundVelocity`, re-read by `UpdateGroundVelocity` at the start of `CharacterController::Update`), and `CharacterController::Update` adds the ground's horizontal velocity only with `InheritGroundVelocity`, none for a light Dynamic ground (M34). `PhysicsWorld::State::ResolveColliderUserData` resolves a sub-shape on a shape.
8. **Scripts and automation.** `CharacterController` has `Move`, `IsGrounded`, `GetGroundNormal` and `GetVelocity` (`Scripting/Bindings/Components.cpp`); `Physics` has its queries, bounds, gravity and masks (`Scripting/Bindings/Physics.cpp`); `physics.bodyInfo` reports `grounded` and `groundNormal` before `tick` (`Automation/Methods/PhysicsMethods.h`).
9. **No committed hash plays a character or a surface.** No scene holds a `ColliderSurface`; FeatureTest's characters are in `CharacterDebris`, `Components`, `Physics` and `Scripting.scene`, none of which a committed replay plays, and no demo holds a character (ADR 0052, the M36 determinism default).

## Decisions

### 1. The `ColliderSurface` component (W36.1)

`ColliderSurfaceComponent` (`Scene/Components/ColliderSurfaceComponent.h`), registry name `ColliderSurface`, version 1, category Physics, appended after `CharacterController` in `BuiltinComponents` and `PhysicsRegistration.cpp` (58 components); requires Transform; default flags (serializable, script-visible, editor-visible, removable). Fields, in this order:

| Field | Type, default | Range | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| `Friction` | `float` 0.5 | ≥ 0, no maximum (RigidBody's) | used instead of the body's friction for contacts with the entity's solid colliders |
| `Restitution` | `float` 0 | [0, 1] | used instead of the body's restitution |
| `SurfaceVelocity` | `vec3` 0 | each component within ±`MaxCharacterSpeed` (500), unit m/s | the velocity of the surface in the entity's own axes (its world rotation, never its scale) |

Both material values always apply while the component is present (no inherit flag); the defaults are RigidBody's, so a component added for its velocity changes nothing else on a body of default material. It applies to every solid collider component on its own entity (Box, Sphere, Capsule, Mesh and TerrainCollider that are not triggers), whichever body gathers it; never to descendants' colliders, triggers, a character's capsule or 2D colliders. RigidBody's and TerrainCollider's `Friction` and `Restitution` descriptions name it (the ledger's material-descriptions row), and `CharacterController.InheritGroundVelocity`'s says a surface carries the character either way.

### 2. The world's surfaces (W36.1)

`Physics/PhysicsWorld.h` (M11's frozen header, a surfaces section reviewed as such) gains:
- `inline constexpr float MaxSurfaceSpeed = 1000.0f` (m/s): the longest body-space velocity the world takes. ADR 0052 named the component's bound (±500 per component); the world bounds the length instead, since a velocity of 500 m/s per component in the entity's axes turns into a body-space vector up to 866 m/s long, whose components would leave ±500.
- `struct PhysicsColliderSurface { uint32_t Collider = 0; float Friction = 0.5f; float Restitution = 0.0f; glm::vec3 SurfaceVelocity{0} }` (the velocity in body axes).
- `Status SetColliderSurfaces(BodyHandle, std::span<const PhysicsColliderSurface>)`: replaces the body's list, kept sorted by collider in its `PhysicsBodyRecord` (`Physics/Private/PhysicsContactListener.h`, `Surfaces`). Errors, with nothing changed: `InvalidArgument` for a handle of no live body, a non-finite value, a `Friction` outside [0, `MaxPhysicsFriction`] (the W36.1 review's finding 1), a `Restitution` outside [0, 1], a `SurfaceVelocity` longer than `MaxSurfaceSpeed`, a collider the body's shape lacks or one listed twice. For each collider of the shape whose effective surface changed (a collider without an entry counts as the body's friction and restitution with no velocity, compared bit for bit), it wakes the bodies inside that collider's bounds (`GetSubShapeBounds`, `WakeBodiesIn`); a list that changes nothing wakes nothing. `SetShape` keeps the entries whose collider the new shape still has; `DestroyBody` drops the record.
- **The listener** (`PhysicsContactListener::ApplySurfaces`, called first in `OnContactAdded` and `OnContactPersisted`, so in the CCD pass too): a sensor contact is left alone; when neither body's record lists surfaces, or neither manifold sub-shape's collider (`ResolvePhysicsCollider`) has one, the contact keeps exactly what Jolt combined; otherwise `mCombinedFriction = sqrt(f1 * f2)` (in double precision, rounded to float: the double product of two floats is exact and its rounded square root the correctly rounded one, a deterministic choice, not an overflow guard), `mCombinedRestitution = max(r1, r2)` and `mRelativeLinearSurfaceVelocity = R2 v2 - R1 v1`, each side's values its surface's or its body's (`Body::GetFriction`, `GetRestitution`; R the body's rotation at that moment, v its surface's velocity, none for a side without a surface). The values depend only on the pair's records and bodies, never on thread order, with `std::sqrt` and `std::max` (no CRT transcendental: a correctly rounded square root).

### 3. The session's surfaces (W36.1)

In a new `Scene/PhysicsSystemSurfaces.cpp` (so M37's merge meets only calls in `PhysicsSystem.cpp`), with its state in `PhysicsSystemState.h`'s M36 region:
- `ColliderSurface`'s construct, update and destroy signals call `OnSurfaceChanged`, which marks the entity in `DirtySurfaces` and never sets `ComponentsChanged`.
- `CollectSurfaces(entry)`: for each solid collider of the body's plan whose entity holds a `ColliderSurface`, its index, `Friction`, `Restitution` and `SurfaceVelocity` turned into the body's axes: the velocity itself for the owner's own colliders, else `conjugate(owner's world rotation) * entity's world rotation * velocity` (`TransformSystem::GetWorldRotation`, rotation only). A sensor body gets none.
- `SendSurfaces(entry)` sends that list (`SetColliderSurfaces`) when it holds any or the body had some (`PhysicsBodyEntry::HasSurfaces`); `CreateBody` and `ReplaceShape` call it for every created and reshaped body, so a gathered collider turned relative to its body (which rebuilds its shape) sends its turned velocity. A refusal, which the component's field bounds make unreachable, is logged and leaves the body's previous list.
- `RefreshSurfaces()`, called by `PreStep` right after its rebuilds and shape refreshes: the bodies holding a marked entity's colliders (`GatheredColliders`, and the entity's own RigidBody or implicit static body), in body handle order, each sent once; the set is emptied. A write in the fixed step is in force at that tick's PreStep, one in the frame phase at the next tick's (§9.2's rule for physics components). `SynchronizeBodies` sends nothing new beyond its created bodies.
- `PhysicsSystem::Create` clears the set after its first rebuild.

### 4. Characters on surfaces (W36.1)

`Physics/CharacterController.h` (M11's frozen header, reviewed as such): `CharacterGroundState` gains `BodyHandle GroundBody` and `uint32_t GroundCollider` (the collider index as a contact on that sub-shape names it), both set by `Update` from Jolt's ground body and sub-shape while the character is grounded (the invalid handle and 0 otherwise), so the ground collider costs no query. The ground collider's surface velocity (the world's record, turned by the ground body's rotation when read) joins the ground's velocity:
- **In `Update`**, read at its start for the ground of the last update: its vertical part is part of the ground's vertical velocity, and its horizontal part is added to the step's velocity while grounded **whatever `InheritGroundVelocity` says** (ADR 0052's choice); with `InheritGroundVelocity` the ground's whole horizontal velocity, surface included, is added as before. A Dynamic body lighter than the character gives none, surface or not (M34's rule). Without a surface the update computes exactly what it did before M36 (no zero is added).
- **In `GetGroundState`**: `GroundVelocity` is Jolt's ground velocity plus the ground collider's surface velocity as the update ended (none on a light body).

`PhysicsCharacterState` (`Scene/PhysicsSystem.h`) gains `UUID GroundCollider`: the collider entity of the ground (`State::ResolveHit` on `GroundBody` and `GroundCollider`: a body's collider table, or a character's entity for its inner body), null when not grounded or when that entity or its body's owner is disabled or pending destruction. `GetCharacterState` and `GetBodyInfo`'s character report fill it through `State::MakeCharacterState` (`PhysicsSystemCharacters.cpp`).

### 5. Script API (W36.1)

- `CharacterController:GetGroundCollider() -> Entity?`, appended after `GetVelocity` in `Bindings/Components.cpp`: the collider entity the last update stood on, nil while not grounded or when it is disabled or destroyed. Read-only; errors as `IsGrounded`'s (no character, physics unavailable).
- `Physics.GetSurface(collider: Entity?) -> SurfaceInfo?` in `RegisterPhysics`, with the alias `SurfaceInfo = {Friction: number, Restitution: number, SurfaceVelocity: vector}`: `PhysicsSystem::GetSurface(entity)` (`PhysicsSystemQueries.cpp`), the values contacts with the entity's solid colliders use: its `ColliderSurface`'s `Friction`, `Restitution` and `SurfaceVelocity` turned into world space by its world rotation, as the component holds them now (the world takes a write at the next PreStep), or else its body's `Friction` and `Restitution` (the settings the body was created with) and a zero velocity; nil for nil (the W36.1 review's finding 2: so `Physics.GetSurface(controller:GetGroundCollider())`, the footing idiom, holds on every tick, nil while the character is in the air), for a disabled entity, and for an entity with no solid collider in a created body (no collider, triggers alone, a character's capsule, 2D colliders, a RigidBody whose colliders are its children's). Read-only; an invalid, destroyed or pending-destruction entity is the argument check's located error.
- Both are read-only, usable in every phase and every run mode, and generate graph nodes (`Call.CharacterController.GetGroundCollider`, `Call.Physics.GetSurface`) without a change to the graph module. `ColliderSurface`'s fields are read and written through the generic proxy. ### The W36.1 review's fixes

The lead's independent review of W36.1 (`1ddd4e91`) found one major finding, three minor ones and two nits; one checkpoint fixes all six:
1. **`MaxPhysicsFriction = 1000.0f`** (`Physics/PhysicsTypes.h`, beside `MaxPhysicsMass`). Jolt's friction solve compares the squared friction impulses with the square of the combined friction times the contact's summed normal impulse (`ContactConstraintManager.cpp`'s `max_linear_lambda`; the CCD pass alike), which overflows a float above about 1.8e19. In MSVC Debug the overflow raises the floating-point exception Jolt unmasks on its workers and aborts the run; elsewhere the clamp becomes infinite. With masses of at most `MaxPhysicsMass` and body speeds of at most the world's 1e6 m/s clamp, a contact's normal impulse in one step stays below about 4e15 N s, so a combined friction of at most 1000 keeps the product below 4e18. `PhysicsWorld::CreateBody` (`BodyDescription::Friction`, documented `[0, MaxPhysicsFriction]` in M11's frozen header, reviewed as such) and `SetColliderSurfaces` refuse a friction above it with `InvalidArgument`, and it is the registry maximum of all four `Friction` fields: `ColliderSurface`'s, `RigidBody`'s, `TerrainCollider`'s and `RigidBody2D`'s, since the 2D world creates its bodies through `PhysicsWorld::CreateBody` too. This is part of M36's ledger row: `PhysicsRegistration.cpp` and `TerrainRegistration.cpp`'s material fields are M36's, and the lead adds `Physics2DRegistration.cpp`, which no other phase 6 milestone touches, to the row at the landing. It adds no field, changes no version and moves no hash: no committed scene, prefab or fixture holds a friction above 1. The Backlog §3 row W36.1 opened is gone, and the listener's double-precision mean stays as a deterministic choice (decision 2), no longer an overflow guard.
2. **`Physics.GetSurface(collider: Entity?)`** takes nil and returns nil, so the footing idiom `Physics.GetSurface(controller:GetGroundCollider())` holds on every tick (decision 5); its description says that a destroyed entity is an error.
3. **The session's tests** of a `ColliderSurface` added to and removed from a collider in play (the construct and destroy signals, the empty list a removal sends), and of a character standing on another (its ground collider the other character's entity).
4. **The world's tests** of what `SetColliderSurfaces` and `SetShape` keep: a refusal leaves the list in force, and `SetShape` keeps the entry of a collider the new shape still has.
5. `CharacterControllerDescription::InheritGroundVelocity`'s comment names the collider surface's carry.
6. `PHYSICS_SURFACE_UNUSED`'s texts name the composition's errors (decision 6).

Tests: "PhysicsWorld: a friction above MaxPhysicsFriction is refused, and the heaviest body steps on the largest" (replacing W36.1's overflow case), "PhysicsRegistration: every Friction field, 3D and 2D, refuses a friction above MaxPhysicsFriction", "PhysicsSystem: a ColliderSurface added in play carries what rests on its collider, and its removal stops it", "PhysicsSystem: a character standing on another character reports that character as its ground collider", the refusal and `SetShape` blocks of "PhysicsWorld: SetColliderSurfaces refuses a bad list and wakes only what rests on a collider whose surface changed", and the binding case's `Physics.GetSurface(nil)` and `Physics.GetSurface(character:GetGroundCollider())` in the air.

### 11. Kinematic bodies push characters: measured (W36.2)

The measuring tests came first, on W36.1's tree (ADR 0052's order): "CharacterController: a Kinematic piston pushes a character at its velocity" (a 0.5 m piston moved by `MoveKinematic` into a standing character, as `PhysicsSystem` moves a Kinematic body in PreStep before the characters update) at 2 and at 8 m/s, and the press and ram cases of decision 12. They showed that `CharacterVirtual`'s moving contact planes already push the character along the contact's normal at the body's velocity: at 2 m/s the piston never entered the capsule, at 8 m/s it overlapped it for at most one tick, by less than its step beyond the look-ahead (3.3 cm at 60 Hz), before the next update pushed the character out, and at both speeds the character then moved at the piston's speed (measured by its position: `CharacterController::GetVelocity`, and so `CharacterController:GetVelocity()`, stays the velocity the update asked for, which a push does not change; `Docs/Backlog.md` §6). So W36.2 adds no wider look-ahead and no extra query for pushes; ADR 0052's fallback is not taken. What is guaranteed, documented and tested: a Kinematic body moving into a character, or a Dynamic one not lighter than it, pushes it at its velocity while it is within the character's look-ahead (0.1 m) at the character's update, so up to 6 m/s at 60 Hz with no overlap; a faster body overlaps the capsule for one tick, by at most its step beyond the look-ahead, and pushes it out at the next update; beyond about 0.1 m plus `Radius` a tick it can pass through (`Docs/Backlog.md` §6).

### 12. The squeeze (W36.2)

`Physics/CharacterController.h` (reviewed as a frozen-interface change): `inline constexpr float CharacterSqueezeTolerance = 0.01f` (m), `struct CharacterSqueeze { bool IsSqueezed = false; BodyHandle Body{}; }` and `CharacterSqueeze CharacterController::GetSqueeze() const`, the last update's. After `Update` (ExtendedUpdate, the controller's own stairs step and the range check), `State::FindSqueeze`:
- **The pushers:** the contacts of the update's last pass (`CharacterVirtual::GetActiveContacts`) that are not sensors or discarded, can push the character (`mCanPushCharacter`, which the recorder turns off for a light Dynamic body, M34), are not Static, and whose point velocity along the contact's normal (which points at the character) exceeds 1 mm/s (`MinPushSpeed`); each with its push over the step (that speed times the step), the largest of its contacts.
- **The measure:** those contacts' distances are the pass's start's, so one `CharacterVirtual::CheckCollision` of the character's capsule against the pushers alone (a body filter), at the position the update left it, reaching as far as the farthest push, gives each pusher's deepest penetration. A pusher squeezes the character when `penetration + padding + push > CharacterSqueezeTolerance`: CharacterVirtual keeps the character `padding` (2 cm) off a surface, so a character the update could keep ahead of the push ends at `-(padding + push)` and is not squeezed, while one held by something else (the floor under a press, a wall behind a piston) ends a push short. `Body` is the squeezing body with the lowest body handle, a deterministic choice (a handle follows the canonical creation order only among the bodies created together; a body created again in play takes a higher one; the W36.2 review's nit 2). The query runs only in an update that met a pusher.
- **The tolerance**, 1 cm: below one tick of a press at 1 m/s (1.6 cm at 60 Hz), above what Jolt's solver leaves of a push it follows (its tolerances are a millimetre). Cases each way (tests): a press brought onto a grounded character and a piston pressing one against a wall are squeezes, the press's from the tick it reaches the bare capsule; a piston pushing a free character, a character carried into a resting ram, the character's own walk into a wall, into a corner and against a steep face, and a press's first contact while still above the head are not.
- **No callback, no escape, no damage (ADR 0052's choice).** A query adds no dispatch path or reserved name; under a flat face the contact normal is vertical, so the engine invents no sideways escape and the press passes into the capsule; the game crushes or shoves what is squeezed (World Tree's Crush hit).
- `PhysicsCharacterState` gains `bool IsSqueezed` and `UUID SqueezedBy` (the squeezing body's owner, never a character's: its inner body pushes nothing; null when not squeezed or when that owner is disabled or pending destruction), filled by `State::MakeCharacterState`.
- **Determinism:** derived from the character's state each update, like `IsGrounded`, and not hashed; the query is a main-thread narrow-phase query whose deepest hit per body does not depend on hit order. It runs in PhysicsPreStep's character update, which M35's world pause holds.

### 13. Script API (W36.2)

`CharacterController:IsSqueezed() -> boolean` and `CharacterController:GetSqueezingBody() -> Entity?`, appended after `GetGroundCollider` in `Bindings/Components.cpp` (the ledger's three methods): the last update's squeeze and the squeezing body's owner (nil while not squeezed, or when it is disabled or destroyed). Read-only, every phase and run mode, so a script sees a squeeze in the next fixed update. `GetGroundCollider` and `GetSqueezingBody` share one binding (`CharacterEntityRead`).

### The W36.2 review's fixes

The lead's independent review of W36.2 (`88dfdbf4`, with `e255510c`'s fixes checked once) found one minor finding and six nits; one checkpoint fixes all seven and makes the lead's decision on the question W36.2's report left open:
- **A character takes a surface velocity's part along the ground alone** (the lead's decision). `CharacterController::Update` projects the ground collider's surface velocity onto the plane of the ground normal (`Utils::AlongGround`, the normal Jolt reports, `up` for a normal that is not usable) before it joins the ground's velocity, so its vertical part is an inclined belt's alone, and the ground state's `GroundVelocity` takes the same projection; a body takes a surface velocity only through friction along the contact (Jolt's `mRelativeLinearSurfaceVelocity`), so a belt whose velocity leaves its surface neither lifts nor presses a character or a body. A velocity already along the ground keeps every bit (a flat belt on a flat floor). `Physics.GetSurface` still reports the component's velocity as it is, of which contacts use the part along the surface. Test: "CharacterController: a belt whose surface velocity leaves its surface carries a character along it without lifting it" (1.5 m/s up and down off a 2.5 m/s belt, with and without `InheritGroundVelocity`: the character stays at its height and grounded and rides 2.5 m in a second), which fails without the projection.
- **Finding 1, a rising lift:** "CharacterController: a character a Kinematic lift carries up is never squeezed, and one it lifts into a ceiling is squeezed by the lift" (1 and 2 m/s, its start and stop included), added before the projection and passing on either side of it. The review's premise, that the character escapes the squeeze only because `Update` adds the ground's vertical velocity, does not hold: with that velocity dropped from the step the case still passes, since `CharacterVirtual`'s moving ground contact pushes the character up a step ahead of the lift as a piston's pushes it sideways (decision 11). The case fails instead when the squeeze's measure over-counts a rising ground's push (a doubled push squeezes every lift rider), which is the regression it guards.
- **Nit 2:** `GetSqueezingBody`, `CharacterSqueeze::Body`, Architecture §9.6 and decision 12 say what the code chooses among several squeezing bodies: the lowest body handle, a deterministic choice (a handle follows the canonical creation order only among the bodies created together).
- **Nit 3:** `CharacterController.h`'s file comment says "inside the character's padded capsule", as the rest does.
- **Nit 4:** `MakeCharacterState`'s branch for a character's inner body as the squeezing body is gone, and decision 12 no longer names it: an inner body is placed, never moved at a velocity of its own, so it pushes nothing (`Docs/Backlog.md` §6).
- **Nits 5 and 6:** the `luau-gameplay` index line and its `characters-and-input` page's opening name the pushes and the squeeze, and the `game-debugging` skill's physics step names `squeezed` and `squeezedBy`. The page also says (the review's follow-up) that the pushing part needs a Kinematic `RigidBody` of its own, and that such a body's Transform leads it by a step within the fixed step, to be compared through `Physics.GetBodyBounds`.
- **Nit 7:** `PhysicsRegistration.cpp`'s file comment is reflowed.
