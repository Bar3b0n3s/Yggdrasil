## Context

Each fact below was read in the code at `7bfd889d`.

- **A scene transition releases every voice.** `PlaySession::State::ConsumeRequests` applies a `Scene.Load` or `Scene.Reload` at the frame's end, after its `AudioUpdate` phase; it takes the mix (`AudioSystem::TakeMix`), destroys the scene's `AudioSystem`, whose destructor stops every source voice and one-shot and unregisters its clips, then makes the new scene's `AudioSystem`, adopts the mix, sets `AudioHeld` and applies the run state, so the new scene's voices are paused until the next `AudioUpdate` (`ApplyAudioRunState`: `AudioHeld || (Paused && !Lockstep)`), and its `Start` plays the `PlayOnStart` sources from cursor 0. Activate makes the first `AudioSystem` the same way, before the startup callbacks, with `AudioHeld` set. The destructor resets the `AudioSystem` (which restores the mix) and only then gives audio time back (`SetAudioTimeOwned(false)`).
- **Voices advance on the time source's frames.** `AudioEngine::State::Pull` reads `ma_engine_read_pcm_frames` in chunks of at most 480 frames and ends with `ReleaseFinishedVoices` (a voice at the end of a non-looping clip, `ma_sound_at_end`). In lockstep and test runs the session pulls exactly round(n × 48000 / F) frames for n ticks in its `AudioUpdate` phase (750 a tick at FeatureTest's 64 Hz); in Host time `Update` pulls each frame's delta; in Device time the device thread reads.
- **No voice fade exists.** `AudioEngine` offers play, stop, pause, settings and transform per voice; `ApplySettings` sets `ma_sound_set_volume(Volume)`. miniaudio's per-voice fader (`ma_sound_set_fade_in_pcm_frames`) is applied in `ma_engine_node_process_pcm_frames__general` to the voice's output frames, after its resampler, only while the fader is not 1 to 1 (`isFadingEnabled`); a fade set from the main thread is stored atomically and taken by the mixer at the voice's next processed frame (`fadeSettings`), and a negative start level means the fader's current one. The fader's cursor is clamped to 32 bits (`ma_fader_process_pcm_frames`: past `UINT_MAX` frames it writes no frame), and its interpolation is `x + (y − x) × a` with `a = (uint32)cursor / (float)(uint32)length` (`ma_fader_get_current_volume`, `ma_mix_f32_fast`); past its end it multiplies by its end level and clips to [−1, 1]. A sound node's clock (`ma_node_get_time`) is an atomic count of the frames the node has output, which advances only while the node is started.
- **Clip registration.** `AudioSystem::State::ResolveClip` and `AcquireClip` load a clip through `GetOrPlaceholder` (the silent clip, registered as the placeholder, for a missing, failed or mistyped one, its diagnostic recorded once; without an asset manager the silent clip with one warning per clip) and register it once per (handle, version) with `RegisterClip`, keeping one reference per registration until the system is destroyed.
- **Priorities.** `AudioSystemEffectPriority` (0) and `AudioSystemMusicPriority` (1) in `AudioSystem.h`; `GetSourcePriority` picks music priority for a `Music`-group source; `PlayOneShot` always starts at effect priority. The pool steals the lowest priority, then the farthest, then the oldest voice.
- **The world pause holds the `Music` group** (M35, ADR 0050 decision 3): `AudioSystem::State::IsHeld(group)` is `Paused || (WorldPaused && group != Ui)`.
- **Scripts and the host.** `IScriptHost` ends with M30's `GetNavigation()`; `Bindings/Audio.cpp`'s `RegisterAudio` registers `PlayOneShot`, `SetGroupVolume` and `GetGroupVolume`; `Bindings/Test.cpp` ends with M28's `Test.SetSaveData`; a binding that changes the host checks `call.PrepareHostMutation()` after validating its arguments. `ScriptApi: every registered function, method, property, operator and constructor has a test` requires every member to be called, in every run mode, by one of the coverage scenarios (`RunAudioBindingsCoverage` for `Audio`).
- **audio.stats** (`AudioMethods.{h,cpp}`) reports the engine, the group volumes and every voice (`AudioVoiceSummary`); a voice's owner is an entity of the play scene or 0 (one-shots, previews). It is read-only, allowed in batches, in the Runtime's subset and not an MCP tool.
- **Audio is never hashed** (`PlaySession::ComputeStateHash`; Architecture §10.2).

### 2. Departures from ADR 0052's design defaults, and the choices it left

Departures, each with its reason:
- **A completed fade is folded into its voice.** ADR 0052 has a fade run on miniaudio's fader. A fade that completes with `Hold` is folded: the fader is switched off (1 to 1) and the voice plays on at the target level through its volume (`Volume × level`). Reason: miniaudio's fader keeps counting frames while it is not 1 to 1 and stops writing frames once its 32-bit cursor reaches `UINT_MAX` (about 24.8 hours at 48 kHz), so a ducked track left playing for a day would then mix an unwritten buffer; folded, the fader runs only while a fade is in progress. The audible difference is nil (the fader's end level and the volume multiply the same frames; the fader's own end clipping to [−1, 1] no longer applies to a held level).
- **The session's music sets each voice's `Volume` to 1 and its level on the fade.** ADR 0052 speaks of a level that fades from where it is; carrying the request's `Volume` on the fade makes a duck, a crossfade and a reclaim one operation (`FadeVoice` from the current level). `audio.stats` therefore reports a music voice's `volume` as 1 and its level as `fadeVolume`.
- **The `Intro` option, `MusicState.Intro`, `TestMusic.Intro` and `InIntro`, and `audio.stats`' `intro`, `next` and `inNext` land with W40.2,** the wave that makes intros play; W40.1's aliases end where W40.1's behaviour ends, and W40.2 appends its members.

Choices ADR 0052 left to the milestone:
- **The same clip whose voice ended starts again.** `PlayMusic` with the clip of the current request keeps its voice while the voice plays; when the voice ended (a clip played once, `Loop` false) or could not start (a full pool), nothing plays to keep, so the call starts it again (ADR 0052: the request stays "until StopMusic or another PlayMusic", and this is another). The brief asks to keep a track "already playing".
- **Reclaim takes the newest voice of the clip that is still fading out**, and the voice that was current fades out over the same `FadeSeconds`.
- **The cap counts the current voice and those fading out** (at most `MaxMusicVoices`, 4); a new voice past it releases the oldest fading voice at once. `StopMusic` moves the current voice among those fading out, so after a stop up to four voices fade out.
- **Errors' texts:** "audio is unavailable" from all four calls where the host has no music (outside a play session: the edit evaluation, fixtures without one); "unknown MusicOptions key '<key>': expected Volume, FadeSeconds or Loop" (a key over 32 bytes is named by its length); "options.<Member> expected <type>, got <type>"; "the music's Volume must be finite and at least 0 (got <v>)"; "the music's fade must be finite and from 0 to 60 seconds (got <v>)". Range checks are `ValidateMusicRequest` and `ValidateMusicFade` (`Scene/SessionMusic.h`), which `SessionMusic::Play` and `Stop` repeat.
- **A fade's frames** are round(seconds × 48000), half up (`GetMusicFadeFrames`), so a 0.25 s fade is exactly 16 ticks at 64 Hz and 15 at 60 Hz.

### 3. Voice fades in the audio engine (W40.1)

`Audio/AudioEngine.h` (M12's frozen interface; a reviewed change):
- **`AudioFadeEnd`** {`Hold`, `Release`} and **`AudioVoiceFade`** `{From = 1, To = 1, Frames = 0}` (linear multipliers of the voice's `Volume`, finite and ≥ 0; `Frames` at most `AudioEngine::MaxFadeFrames`, `0xFFFFFFFF`, miniaudio's fader length; the default is no fade).
- **`AudioVoiceDescription::Fade`** (appended): the voice's starting fade, set before `ma_sound_start`, so no frame plays at another level; a voice that starts paused holds it. `Frames` 0 sets the level at once.
- **`Status FadeVoice(AudioVoiceHandle, float target, uint64_t frames, AudioFadeEnd end = Hold)`**: from the level the voice has reached to `target` over `frames`, replacing a fade in progress. 0 frames: the level at once, and `Release` releases the voice at once. Errors: `NotFound` for a stale or null handle; `InvalidArgument` for a target that is not finite or below 0, more than `MaxFadeFrames` frames, or an `AudioFadeEnd` outside the enum.
- **`AudioVoiceInfo`** gains (appended) `FadeVolume` (the level now), `FadeTarget` (the level a fade in progress goes to, else the level) and `FadeEnd` (`Hold` once no fade is in progress).
- **The clock.** A fade counts its voice's node clock (`ma_node_get_time`, atomic, read from the main thread whoever mixes): the frames the mixer has output of the voice, which a paused voice holds. A fade set between pulls starts at the voice's next mixed frame, which is the clock's value when it was set, so the engine's record of the fade (from, to, frames, start, end) gives the level miniaudio's fader has: computed with the fader's own formula, exact while the engine pulls its own frames (Simulation and Host time), within a device period while the device reads. `FadeVoice` passes that level as the fade's explicit start, never miniaudio's "current level" (−1), so a fade set before a voice's first mixed frame starts from the starting fade's level.
- **Ends.** Every pull ends with `CompleteFades` before `ReleaseFinishedVoices`, and so does `Update` (Device time): a `Release` fade's voice is released at the end of the pull that completes it, a `Hold` fade is folded into the voice (decision 2). A voice that never faded never touches the fader: `ApplySettings` multiplies its `Volume` by its level, 1, exactly.

### 4. The clip registrations, shared (W40.1)

`AudioSystem`'s `ResolveClip` and `AcquireClip` move, unchanged, into a private class of the Scene module, `Scene/Private/AudioClipRegistry.{h,cpp}` (`Resolve`, `Acquire`, `Clear`; its owner's name in its logs), which `AudioSystem` and `SessionMusic` each hold one of, so the music's registrations are its own and a scene's `AudioSystem` going never drops them (ADR 0052's "duplicates `AudioSystem`'s rule", shared rather than copied). Its engine may be null (a session without one, decision 5): it then resolves clips and registers none (`Acquire` is `InvalidState`). `AudioSystem`'s behaviour, logs and destruction order (voices, then clips, then the mix) are unchanged; its constructor verifies its engine (`ENGINE_CORE_VERIFY`), since it now dereferences it in the member initializer.

### 5. The session's music and its lifecycle (W40.1)

- **`Scene/SessionMusic.{h,cpp}`**: `SessionMusic(SessionMusicSpecification{Audio, Assets})`, `Status Play(const MusicRequest&)`, `Status Stop(double fadeSeconds)`, `const std::optional<MusicState>& GetState()`, `SetPaused`, `IsPaused`, `Update()` (after the frame's pulls: forget released voices, take the snapshot), `const MusicVoices& GetVoices()` (the snapshot) and `MusicVoices Inspect()` (now); `MusicRequest {Clip, Volume = 1, FadeSeconds = 0, Loop = true}`, `MusicState {Clip, Volume, Loop}`, `MusicVoice {Voice, Clip}` (the clip the voice was started for, the request's even when a missing clip plays the placeholder), `MusicVoices {Current, CurrentInfo, FadingOut}`; `MaxMusicFadeSeconds` 60 and `MaxMusicVoices` 4 (engine constants, the ledger's settings row). Every voice is non-spatial, in the `Music` group, at `AudioSystemMusicPriority`, `Owner` 0, `Volume` 1. The voices of decision 2's rules: keep (fade to `Volume` from the current level, `Loop` applied), crossfade (the current voice fades to 0 with `Release`; at once for 0), reclaim, the cap, stop. An engine failure (registration, a full pool of higher priorities) is logged as a warning and leaves no current voice; the request stands. **Without an engine** (`Audio` null) the music validates and records the requests and starts no voice (`GetVoices` and `Inspect` empty): the feature host's scratch sessions, which discover test suites by starting each suite's scene without audio, run that scene's startup callbacks, and a track asked for in `OnStart` (FeatureTest's `MusicConductor`, every World Tree scene) must neither raise there nor behave differently from a session with audio (the unit case "FeatureTest: the Timing suite passes with 0, 1, 2 and 5 steps per frame" discovers the `Music` suite in an editor without audio).
- **`PlaySession`** (its audio regions, ADR 0052's ledger): `Activate` makes the music in every Play session (with or without an engine; none in Simulate, which runs no scripts), after the first `AudioSystem`, before the startup callbacks, and sets `MusicHeld`; `ApplyAudioRunState` pauses the music while `MusicHeld || (Paused && !Lockstep)`, in its order (pause before time goes back, resume after it is taken); the first `UpdateAudio` clears `AudioHeld` and `MusicHeld`; a scene transition sets `AudioHeld` only, so the music is never held at a doorway (in Device time a held voice would be silent from the swap to the next frame's `AudioUpdate`); `UpdateAudio` ends with `SessionMusic::Update` after the pulls; the destructor resets the music before the `AudioSystem` (which restores the mix) and before time goes back. The world pause never reaches it (`ApplyWorldPauseToAudio` addresses the `AudioSystem` only). `PlaySession::GetMusic()` (const and not) returns it: null in Simulate and before `Activate`.

### 6. Script API (W40.1)

- **`Audio.PlayMusic(clip: AssetRef, options: MusicOptions?) -> ()`**, `MusicOptions = {Volume: number?, FadeSeconds: number?, Loop: boolean?}` (an alias; no other key is accepted; read raw); **`Audio.StopMusic(fadeSeconds: number?) -> ()`**; **`Audio.GetMusic() -> MusicState?`**, `MusicState = {Clip: AssetRef, Volume: number, Loop: boolean}` (read, `Mutates` false). All in `RegisterAudio`, after `GetGroupVolume`; the module's description says it holds the session's music. `PlayMusic` and `StopMusic` validate, then `PrepareHostMutation` (refused in a read-only evaluation; an evaluation's call invalidates an input recording), then act; callable in every callback, phase, task and test case; a call takes effect at once in the engine, so its voice starts (or fades) with the next pulled frame, the frame's `AudioUpdate`.
- **`Test.GetMusic() -> TestMusic?`**, `TestMusic = {Voice: string, Clip: AssetRef, CursorFrames: number, LengthFrames: number, FadeVolume: number, FadingOut: number}`, appended after `Test.SetSaveData` (test runs only, `Mutates` false): the snapshot of the last `AudioUpdate` phase, nil without a current voice; `Voice` is the engine handle as 16 lowercase hexadecimal digits. "TestBindings: every registered member has its frozen availability and mutation metadata" lists it.
- **`IScriptHost::GetMusic()`** (`Scripting/ScriptHost.h`), appended after `GetNavigation` with the default null; the play session's host returns its music; `ScriptTestFixture` gains a `Music` member its override returns (null by default, as in an edit evaluation).

### 7. Automation (W40.1)

No new method (125 methods and 86 proxied tools unchanged; agents drive the music through `script.eval` in the play context). `audio.stats` (`AudioMethods.{h,cpp}`, M12's frozen wire format, appended): `AudioVoiceSummary.fadeVolume` (every voice's level, 1 for one that never faded) and `AudioStatsResult.music`, the new registry struct `AudioMusicSummary {clip, volume, loop, voice, fadingVoices}` (the current request, its voice's handle, empty when it ended or could not start, and the handles fading out, oldest first; empty, `volume` 0 and `loop` false, without music and outside a Play session), read live through `PlaySession::GetMusic` (`SessionMusic::Inspect`). The method's description names the music.

### 8. Determinism

Audio is never hashed. `Audio.GetMusic` returns only the requests scripts made, a pure function of the run's calls (like the load parameters, which survive `Scene.Load` unhashed), so a script that branches on it is deterministic in live play, lockstep, test runs and replays. The voice's progress (cursor, level, the voices fading out) is readable only through `Test.GetMusic` (test runs own audio time) and `audio.stats`. The engine's own failures are logged, never raised, so no script's flow depends on the pool. Fades follow the time source, as cursors do: simulation time in lockstep, test runs and replays. No committed hash moves.

## Risks

- **Fades read the mixer's clock.** The record's level matches miniaudio's fader only while the engine pulls its own frames; in Device time a fade's start level and its release are within a device period of the fader's (Backlog §6). A releasing fade then ends at a level near 0.
- **The session's music under a full pool.** Music voices take music priority, so a pool full of Music sources, music voices and previews refuses a new track (logged); FadingOut voices count toward the four, and a new track releases the oldest of them first.
- **Shared session files.** `PlaySession.{h,cpp}`, `ScriptHost.h` and `ScriptTestFixture.h` are M38's too (disjoint regions: the locale's state, hash block and replay header against the audio lifecycle); the later lander keeps the other's hunks.
