## Context

Each fact below was read in the code at `7bfd889d`.

- **No joint exists.** No first-party file creates a Jolt constraint. Jolt 5.6.0 is vendored whole with every `Constraints/*.cpp` compiled under `JPH_CROSS_PLATFORM_DETERMINISTIC` (`Vendor/JoltPhysics/VENDOR.md`): `HingeConstraint`, `SliderConstraint`, `DistanceConstraint` and `FixedConstraint`, each created from its settings with `Create(Body&, Body&)` in world space or relative to the bodies' centres of mass (`EConstraintSpace::LocalToBodyCOM`), `Body::sFixedToWorld` as the world (an `EmptyShape` at the origin, its centre of mass 0).
- **Jolt has no breaking.** A constraint reports each part's accumulated impulse of the last collision step (`GetTotalLambdaPosition`, `GetTotalLambdaRotation`, `GetTotalLambdaRotationLimits`, `GetTotalLambdaPositionLimits`, `GetTotalLambdaMotor`); nothing in `Constraints/` removes one.
- **Asserts content can reach** (each refused or made safe by decision 2): equal hinge or slider limits without a limit spring (`HingeConstraint.cpp:83`, `SliderConstraint.cpp:159`); hinge limits outside `[-pi, 0]` and `[0, pi]` (`HingeConstraint.cpp:158-159`); slider limits of the wrong sign (`SliderConstraint.cpp:185-186`); hinge axes not unit within 1e-5 at the solve (`HingeRotationConstraintPart.h:78-79`); `SetMotorState` with settings that are not `IsValid` (`MotorSettings.h:66`); a position drive whose spring has no frequency (`AxisConstraintPart.h:231`, `SpringPart.h:100`); `DistanceConstraint::SetDistance` with a minimum above the maximum (`DistanceConstraint.h:76`). A spring of an unbounded frequency overflows `k = m omega^2` to infinity and its bias to NaN (`SpringPart.h`).
- **A constraint holds raw body pointers** (`TwoBodyConstraint.h:61-62`, `GetBody1`, `GetBody2`), and the session destroys bodies on every RigidBody change, refusal, disable and destroy flush and at its end (`PhysicsSystem.cpp`: `Rebuild`, `ReplaceShape`, `RemoveBody`, `FlushDestroyed`, the destructor). `PhysicsWorld::DestroyBody` removed and destroyed the Jolt body and nothing else.
- **A replaced shape moves the centre of mass.** `PhysicsWorld::SetShape` calls `BodyInterface::SetShape(..., false, ...)`, whose `Body::SetShapeInternal` keeps the body's origin and moves its centre of mass by the shapes' difference; a constraint learns it only through `Constraint::NotifyShapeChanged(BodyID, deltaCOM)` ("bodies don't know which constraints are connected to them").
- **Solve order follows the add-and-remove history.** `ConstraintManager::Add` appends, `Remove` moves the last constraint into the hole, and each island's constraints are sorted by `(priority, mConstraintIndex)` (`ConstraintManager.cpp:103-115`, called from `PhysicsSystem.cpp:1503` under `mDeterministicSimulation`); the large-island splitter takes that sorted order.
- **A constraint is solved only while one of its bodies is Dynamic and one is awake** (`TwoBodyConstraint::IsActive`, `BuildIslands`).
- **The body-pair hook.** `PhysicsWorld::Create` installs `Detail::CollideBodiesWithEdgeRemoval` as `PhysicsSystem::SetSimCollideBodyVsBody` (a `std::function`), the one place that sees both bodies of a pair before the narrow phase. Jolt's continuous collision casts of LinearCast bodies (`JobFindCCDContacts`, `CCDBroadPhaseCollector`) do not call it; they test the collision groups and then cast through the system's simulation shape filter (`SetSimShapeFilter`, consulted by both passes through `SimShapeFilterWrapper`), which is null by default. The collision groups' filter sees only groups (`PhysicsCollisionGroups.h`).
- **The session's step.** `PhysicsSystem::PreStep` rebuilds bodies in canonical order when `ComponentsChanged` or the hierarchy changed (`Rebuild`, which composes with `ComposePhysicsBodies` and keeps nothing of it) and refreshes shapes otherwise; `SynchronizeBodies` runs the same rebuild before every start flush when `ComponentsChanged`; `PostStep` writes back and dispatches sorted pair events with `Dispatching` set; `FlushDestroyed` closes the pairs of entities that left play and removes their bodies, in passes over `FlushCandidates`; `AppendStateHash` writes each body's and character's owner, pose, velocities and sleep flag in canonical order and returns early without bodies or characters. Component signals (`ConnectComponentSignals`) set `ComponentsChanged`, which recomposes the scene, so a per-tick write of a signalled component would recompose every tick.
- **Entities.** `Scene::FindEntityByID` returns an invalid handle for an entity pending destruction, `FindReadableEntityByID` a readable one until the flush; `IsPresent` is "found and not `HierarchyDisabledTag`"; `ComposePhysicsBodies` skips an entity with `DisabledTag` on it or an ancestor.
- **Diagnostics.** `PhysicsSystem::State::RaiseDiagnostic` records each (code, severity, entity, subject) once and logs it at Error or Warn; the listener hears it, and `ScriptEngine::OnPhysicsDiagnostic` publishes it as a script error whatever its code. M11's `PhysicsDiagnosticCodes` is frozen and must stay contiguous after `PREFAB_MISSING_ASSET` (`ProjectValidatorTests.cpp`); M21's `Physics2DDiagnosticCodes` (`Scene/Physics2DValidation.h`) is the precedent of a code array of its own appended at the end of `ProjectValidator.cpp`'s tables. `ValidateScenePhysics` is frozen (`PhysicsValidation.h`), so the validator composes the joints itself.
- **Registry ranges.** `FieldMeta::Min`, `Max` and `MinMagnitude` apply per numeric component and are enforced on every write path; `MinMagnitude` bounds each component, not a vector's length, so it cannot refuse a zero axis.

## Decisions

### 1. The components and their enums (W37.1)

- **Four components,** `HingeJoint`, `SliderJoint`, `DistanceJoint` and `FixedJoint` (version 1 each), in the sixteenth category **Joints** (`Scene/Registration/JointsRegistration.cpp`, `RegisterJointsComponents` after `RegisterNavigationComponents`; `BuiltinComponents` appends them after `NavModifier`). Each requires Transform and excludes the other three, so an entity holds at most one joint; all are script-visible, editor-visible and removable (the default flags), every field scriptable, `RunModes::All`. Headers: `Scene/Components/HingeJointComponent.h` (with the enum), `SliderJointComponent.h`, `DistanceJointComponent.h`, `FixedJointComponent.h`.
- **Registry enums,** registered by `RegisterJointsComponents`: `JointMotorMode` {Off, Velocity, Position} (`HingeJointComponent.h`, used by the hinge and the slider) and `JointType` {Hinge, Slider, Distance, Fixed} (the world's `PhysicsJointType`, `Physics/PhysicsJoint.h`; `physics.jointInfo`'s `type`).
- **Fields,** in this order (every one with a description; angles in degrees, distances in metres, forces in newtons, torques in newton metres):

  | Field | Hinge | Slider | Distance | Fixed | Type, default, registry range |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | `ConnectedBody` | yes | yes | yes | yes | EntityRef, null (the world) |
  | `Anchor` | yes | yes | yes | yes | vec3 (0, 0, 0), each component in ±1e9 (`MaxPhysicsCoordinate`) |
  | `ConnectedAnchor` | | | yes | | vec3 (0, 0, 0), ±1e9 |
  | `Axis` | yes | yes | | | vec3 (1, 0, 0), no range (a zero axis is `PHYSICS_JOINT_INVALID`'s) |
  | `UseLimits` | yes | yes | | | bool, false |
  | `LowerAngle` / `UpperAngle` | yes | | | | float −90 / 90, [−180, 0] / [0, 180] |
  | `LowerLimit` / `UpperLimit` | | yes | | | float −1 / 1, [−1e9, 0] / [0, 1e9] |
  | `MinDistance` / `MaxDistance` | | | yes | | float 0 / 1, [0, 1e9] each |
  | `Frequency` / `Damping` | | | yes | | float 0 / 0, [0, 1000] each (0 Hz is rigid) |
  | `MaxFrictionTorque` / `MaxFrictionForce` | yes | yes | | | float 0, ≥ 0 |
  | `Motor` | yes | yes | | | `JointMotorMode`, Off |
  | `MotorTargetVelocity` | yes | yes | | | float 0, [−1e6, 1e6] deg/s or m/s |
  | `MotorTargetAngle` / `MotorTargetPosition` | yes | yes | | | float 0, [−180, 180] deg / [−1e6, 1e6] m |
  | `MotorMaxTorque` / `MotorMaxForce` | yes | yes | | | float 1000, [0, 1e12] |
  | `MotorFrequency` | yes | yes | | | float 2, [0.001, 1000] Hz |
  | `MotorDamping` | yes | yes | | | float 1, [0, 1000] |
  | `CollideConnected` | yes | yes | yes | yes | bool, false |
  | `BreakForce` | yes | yes | yes | yes | float 0, ≥ 0 (0 never breaks) |
  | `BreakTorque` | yes | yes | | yes | float 0, ≥ 0 (0 never breaks) |
  | `Broken` | yes | yes | yes | yes | bool, false |

- **Departures from ADR 0052's ranges, with their reason** (§9.1, nothing invalid reaches Jolt): ADR 0052 gives `Anchor`, `ConnectedAnchor`, the slider's limits, the distances, the springs and the velocity and position targets no maximum, and `MotorFrequency` "> 0". A registry minimum is inclusive, so "> 0" is `MotorFrequency` ≥ 0.001 Hz (`MinPhysicsJointMotorFrequency`); an unbounded spring frequency or damping overflows Jolt's spring constants to infinity and its bias to NaN, so both are at most 1000 (`MaxPhysicsJointSpring`, far stiffer than a 60 Hz step follows); targets are at most 1e6 in magnitude (`MaxPhysicsJointMotorTarget`), so a motor's impulse stays finite; anchors, slider limits and distances stay within the physics range (`MaxPhysicsCoordinate`), as every position physics places does. Each bound is far beyond any game; the world checks the same bounds (decision 2), so a value written past the registry (a hand-made C++ component) is refused there.
- **What is not a write error.** A zero `Axis`, equal limits while `UseLimits` holds and a `MinDistance` above `MaxDistance` span fields or entities; they are `PHYSICS_JOINT_INVALID` (decision 9), never refused writes, so a script may set a joint's fields in any order.
- **Hinge and slider sense.** The hinge turns the joint's body about `Axis` (positive by the right-hand rule) relative to what it is joined to; the slider moves it along `Axis`. A fixed joint's frame uses its entity's X axis.

### 2. The world's joints (W37.1; `Physics/PhysicsJoint.h`, `PhysicsWorld.h`'s joints section, `Physics/PhysicsWorldJoints.cpp`)

- **Types** (`Physics/PhysicsJoint.h`): `JointHandle` (a `uint32_t` the world hands out from 1 in creation order and never reuses; 0 is invalid); `PhysicsJointType`; `PhysicsJointMotor` {Off, Velocity, Position}; `PhysicsJointFrame {Point, Axis, Normal}` in a body's space relative to its origin (world space for the world); `PhysicsJointSettings {CollideConnected, UseLimits, LowerLimit, UpperLimit, LimitFrequency, LimitDamping, MaxFriction, Motor, MotorTargetVelocity, MotorTargetPosition, MotorMaxForce, MotorFrequency, MotorDamping}` in Jolt's units (radians, metres), the distance's minimum and maximum in `LowerLimit` and `UpperLimit`; `PhysicsJointDescription {Type, Body, ConnectedBody, Frame, ConnectedFrame, Settings, Priority}`; `PhysicsJointState {Value, Force, Torque, Point, ConnectedPoint, Axis}` (the last three in world space: where the bodies hold the joint's two points now, and a hinge's or slider's axis as the connected body holds it, zero for the others; what the debug view draws in play); the bounds `MaxPhysicsJointMotorTarget` (1e6), `MaxPhysicsJointMotorForce` (1e12), `MinPhysicsJointMotorFrequency` (0.001), `MaxPhysicsJointSpring` (1000); the constant factors `JointDegreesToRadians` and `JointRadiansToDegrees` (no CRT transcendental, §9.1).
- **API** (`PhysicsWorld.h`, M11's frozen header, appended as one section, reviewed as a change to a frozen interface): `Result<JointHandle> CreateJoint(const PhysicsJointDescription&)`, `void DestroyJoint(JointHandle)` (a dead handle ignored), `bool IsJointValid(JointHandle)`, `Status SetJointSettings(JointHandle, const PhysicsJointSettings&)` (in place, waking the bodies), `void SetJointPriority(JointHandle, uint32_t)`, `PhysicsJointState GetJointState(JointHandle)` (zero for a dead handle), `uint32_t GetJointCount()`.
- **Jolt's sides.** Jolt's body 1 is the connected body (`Body::sFixedToWorld` for the world) and its body 2 the joint's body, so a hinge's angle and a slider's position are the joint's body's motion relative to what it is joined to. The world converts the frames from the bodies' origins to their centres of mass (subtracting `Shape::GetCenterOfMass`) and creates the constraint in `LocalToBodyCOM` space; Jolt computes a hinge's inverse initial orientation from the local frames, so frames formed in world space with equal axes on both sides read angle 0 at that pose.
- **Refused or made safe before Jolt** (`InvalidArgument`, nothing created or changed): an unknown type or motor; a `Body` that names no live body, a `ConnectedBody` neither invalid nor live, the same body twice; a frame point not finite or beyond `MaxPhysicsCoordinate`; an axis or normal not unit within `PhysicsUnitRotationTolerance`, or a normal more than 1e-3 from perpendicular (the cosine); a setting that is not finite or outside the bounds above; a hinge's or slider's limits of the wrong sign, or equal limits while `UseLimits` holds (hinge limits compared after the clamp). Made safe: hinge limits are clamped to Jolt's [−pi, 0] and [0, pi] and a hinge's position target to [−pi, pi]; the axes are normalized before Jolt sees them; without `UseLimits` a hinge gets (−pi, pi) and a slider (−FLT_MAX, FLT_MAX), Jolt's "no limits"; the motor's settings are set before `SetMotorState`, and the limits before the targets, so a target is clamped to the new limits as Jolt clamps it.
- **Bodies first.** `DestroyBody` destroys the body's joints (removing their constraints, waking the bodies they joined) before it removes the body; `SetShape` calls `NotifyShapeChanged` with the centres of mass' difference for every joint of the body (the world's frames are relative to origins, so nothing the world keeps moves); the destructor removes every constraint before the bodies.
- **Measuring.** `Step` records its collision step's duration (`deltaTime / collisionSteps`). `GetJointState` returns the hinge's `GetCurrentAngle` (Jolt's own deterministic trigonometry), the slider's `GetCurrentPosition`, the distance between the two points from the bodies' current centre-of-mass transforms, 0 for a fixed joint; the force is the length of the linear impulses (hinge: the point part; slider: its two position parts and the limit part; distance: its axis part; fixed: the point part) and the torque that of the angular ones (hinge: its two rotation parts and the limit part; slider and fixed: the rotation part), never the motor's or the friction's, each divided by the collision step's duration.

### 3. Which bodies a joint joins (W37.1; `Scene/PhysicsJoints.{h,cpp}`)

- **`ComposePhysicsJoints(scene, bodies)`,** a pure function over `ComposePhysicsBodies`' result, never changing it, shared by the session, the validator and (W37.2) the debug draw. Over effectively enabled entities (no `DisabledTag` on the entity or an ancestor), in canonical order, it lists a `PhysicsJointPlan {Entity, Type, Body, Connected, ConnectedOrigin, IsCreatable}` per joint and its `PHYSICS_JOINT_INVALID` diagnostics sorted by (entity, code, subject).
- **The joint's body** is the body of the RigidBody at or above the joint's entity, walking up: a RigidBody ends the walk; a CharacterController or a RigidBody2D met first is invalid ("character", "2d"). **`ConnectedBody`** resolves by the same walk, or, without a RigidBody at or above it, to the implicit static body its own solid colliders form: the one it owns, or the one an ancestor gathers them into; an entity without either is invalid ("connected"). Null joins the body to the world.
- **Not creatable, without a diagnostic:** an end whose body plan a body diagnostic refused (its plan is not creatable while it has colliders), and a `ConnectedBody` whose entity is disabled (it joins again when that entity is enabled, as a body comes back with its entity). A RigidBody without a collider makes no body, without a diagnostic of its own, so a joint on it is invalid ("body"/"connected": "makes no body").
- **The component read once** (`ReadPhysicsJoint`): `PhysicsJointFields {Type, ConnectedBody, Anchor, Axis, ConnectedAnchor, Settings, BreakForce, BreakTorque, Broken}` in the world's units; an entity that holds several joint components anyway (a hand-written file) is read through the first in `BuiltinComponents` order. A runtime entity marked for destruction is read until the flush.
- **Frames from the scene** (`MakePhysicsJointWorldFrame`, `ToPhysicsJointBodyFrame`, `ToPhysicsJointWorldFrame`): `Anchor` through the entity's world matrix (scale baked in, as for colliders), the axis through its 3x3 part and normalized, and the normal made by crossing the axis with the world axis least aligned with it, so no rotation and no CRT transcendental is needed; into a body's space by its pose's inverse, the axes normalized and the normal made perpendicular again (Gram-Schmidt) after the rotation.

### 4. Collide connected (W37.1; `Physics/Private/PhysicsEdgeRemoval.{h,cpp}`)

- The world counts the joints whose `CollideConnected` is false between each body pair (`Detail::PhysicsJointedPairs`, keyed by the two `BodyID` values with their sequence numbers, the lower first; a joint to the world counts nothing). Its body-pair hook (`Detail::CollideBodiesUnlessJointed`, installed as `SimCollideBodyVsBody` by `PhysicsWorld::Create`) drops such a pair before the narrow phase, so no contact, contact record or collision event is ever made between the two bodies.
- **A refinement of ADR 0052, with its reason:** Jolt's continuous collision casts do not call the hook, so a LinearCast body would still hit the body it is joined to. The world also installs `Detail::PhysicsJointedPairFilter` as its simulation shape filter while it holds a jointed pair (null otherwise, so a world without one collides exactly as before), which refuses every shape of such a pair in both passes. "PhysicsWorld: a joint's bodies never collide while CollideConnected is false, continuous casts included" covers both.

### 5. The joints in the session (W37.1; `Scene/PhysicsSystemJoints.cpp`, hooks in `PhysicsSystem.cpp`)

- **Signals.** The four components' signals mark the joints (`JointsChanged`, and the changed joint entity), never `ComponentsChanged`, so a per-tick write never recomposes the bodies; a construction or a destruction marks the plan stale.
- **Synchronization** (`SynchronizeJoints`): at the end of every `Rebuild` (after every body of the pass, over the composition `Rebuild` now keeps), in `PreStep` after its rebuilds and in `SynchronizeBodies` (which now also runs when only joints changed), whenever a joint component changed or a body was created or removed. The plan is composed again only when the bodies were rebuilt, a joint component was added or removed, or a changed joint's key (type, `ConnectedBody`, `Anchor`, `Axis`, `ConnectedAnchor`, `UseLimits` and the limits) differs from what its last plan read; anything else (a motor target, a threshold, `Broken`) uses the plan it has. Then, for each joint entity in UUID order: a joint whose entity is leaving play keeps what it has until `FlushDestroyed`; a refused, `Broken` or not-listed joint is not in force; one whose two bodies exist is created, or, when it lives with the same bodies, has its settings applied in place (`SetJointSettings`) if they changed; one whose end has no body is not in force; one whose connected end (or either end's body) is leaving play, disabled or destroyed this tick, keeps what it has until `FlushDestroyed`, and a reason its plan then has is raised only after the flush, which composes the plans again (`RemoveLeavingJoints`). An axis the joint entity's scale makes vanish raises `PHYSICS_JOINT_INVALID` with Subject "frame", and a description or settings the world refuses (values past the registry's ranges, which only a write that bypasses the registry can bring) with Subject "refused".
- **Kept frames.** A hinge's, slider's or fixed joint's two frames are formed when it is first created: the joint entity's world frame (decision 3) expressed in each body's space from the scene's poses of that moment (the owners' world translation and rotation), so the authored pose is the hinge's angle 0 and the slider's position 0. They are kept, relative to the bodies' origins, only once the creation succeeds (a refused creation keeps nothing, so a fix forms them afresh), and the joint is created from them whenever a body is created again, so a door that swung keeps its zero and its limits. They are formed again when the joint's formation differs: its type, the bodies its ends resolve to, `ConnectedBody`, `Anchor`, `Axis`, or the chain of entities from the joint's entity up to its body's owner (a reparent). A local transform change of an entity on that chain does not form them again. A distance joint keeps nothing: its two anchors (`Anchor` in its entity's space, `ConnectedAnchor` in `ConnectedBody`'s entity's space or world space) are read from the scene at every creation, and a distance joint in force whose formation differs (the same fields with `ConnectedAnchor`) is made again, so a new anchor applies at the next synchronization (the W37.1 review's finding 1). Every other field of the four components is a setting applied in place (decision 2) or read by the break pass. Frames go with the joint component or a destroyed entity; a disabled entity keeps them for its return. **ADR 0052's "SetShape moves the kept frames' centre-of-mass offsets"** is met by keeping the frames relative to the origins: nothing the session keeps moves, and the world tells the live constraint (decision 2).
- **Priorities.** Every joint in force solves at its entity's rank in UUID order among the joints in force (0, 1, ...), refreshed whenever the joints in force change (a creation, a removal, a break), so the solve order is a function of the state and never of Jolt's add-and-remove history.
- **Bodies first, in the session too.** `RemoveBody` destroys the body's joints before `PhysicsWorld::DestroyBody` (which would too) on every path that calls it: a RigidBody change, a refusal after an edit, the destroy and disable flushes; the destructor destroys every joint before the bodies.
- **The flush.** `FlushDestroyed` removes the live joints of the entities that left play, in each of its passes: a destroyed entity's entry goes with its frames, a disabled one's keeps its frames. When it returns, no entity pending destruction or disabled has a joint in force.
- **The state hash** (the ledger's joint part): after the bodies and characters, inside `PhysicsSystem::AppendStateHash`, for every joint whose frames the session keeps, in canonical order of its entity: the entity's UUID (little endian) and the bits of its two kept frames (point, axis and normal on the joint's body, then on the connected body, as little-endian float bit patterns). Nothing without one, so no committed hash moves and `PlaySession::ComputeStateHash` does not change. The early return without bodies or characters now also needs no kept frame, since a disabled joint entity keeps its frames while its body is gone.
- **Determinism.** Every joint field, `Broken` included, is component data in the canonical serialization and the hash. Joints are created in UUID order after the bodies of each pass; break events are in UUID order; no unordered container is iterated for output.

### 6. Breaking (W37.1)

- At the start of `PostStep` (`BreakJoints`), for every joint in force whose entity is present and whose `BreakForce` or `BreakTorque` is set, in UUID order, the world's reading of the step that just ran is compared: a force above `BreakForce` or a torque above `BreakTorque` breaks it: it is destroyed (its bodies woken), `Broken = true` is written into its component directly (a runtime system's write, §5.1: no signal, and scene data, so hashed) and a `PhysicsJointBreak {Joint, Force, Torque, Tick}` is recorded (`GetLastJointBreaks`, cleared at each `PostStep`). After that `PostStep`'s pair events, each break whose joint entity is still present is dispatched to the listener (`IPhysicsEventListener::OnPhysicsJointBreak`, defaulted, so every other listener is unchanged), with `Dispatching` set. A joint whose entity is leaving play does not break (its component cannot be written any more); it goes at the flush.
- A `Broken` joint is never created; `Broken` written false mends it at the next synchronization, at its kept frames (the solver pulls the bodies back together). `Broken` written true by a script removes the joint without a break event.
- The force is the last collision step's: below 60 Hz there are several collision steps a tick, so a joint at the edge of its threshold may break on another tick than at another rate (deterministic in every configuration; ADR 0052's risk). The descriptions say so.

### 7. The joint reads (W37.1; scripts and automation in W37.2)

- `PhysicsSystem::GetJointInfo(UUID) -> std::optional<PhysicsJointReport>`: `{Entity, Type, Body, Connected, Created, Broken, Angle (degrees), Position (m), Distance (m), Force (N), Torque (N m), Motor, Point, ConnectedPoint, Axis}` (the last three the world's, for the debug view), nullopt when the entity does not exist or holds no joint component; `Created` false for a refused, broken, disabled or not yet created joint (or one whose entity is leaving play), and the readings zero then; each type's reading in its own member, the others zero; read live from the world, so the state after the last step and a teleport since. `GetLastJointBreaks() -> std::span<const PhysicsJointBreak>`.

### 9. Validation (W37.1)

- **`PHYSICS_JOINT_INVALID`** (Error, not fixable), `PhysicsJointInvalidCode` in its own array `PhysicsJointDiagnosticCodes` in `Scene/PhysicsJoints.h` (never M11's), appended at the end of `ProjectValidator.cpp`'s `ValidatorCodes` and `ValidatorCodeInfos` (after M36's `PHYSICS_SURFACE_UNUSED` at the merge, the ledger's order); `ProjectValidatorTests.cpp`'s count line grows by the array, and its order case requires it after `NAV_MESH_STALE` and at the end. `CheckPhysics` adds `ComposePhysicsJoints` over the scene's composition to `ValidateScenePhysics`' diagnostics. One per joint, the first reason in this order, each with its Subject (the validator's diagnostic id reads it): the joint's end first, no RigidBody at or above the joint's entity or its RigidBody makes no body ("body"), or the end under a CharacterController ("character") or a RigidBody2D ("2d"); then the connected end, a `ConnectedBody` naming no entity of the scene or resolving to no body ("connected"), or under a CharacterController ("character") or a RigidBody2D ("2d"); then both ends the same body ("same"); a zero `Axis` (length below 1e-6; "axis"); `UseLimits` with equal limits ("limits", on `UpperAngle` or `UpperLimit`); a `MinDistance` above `MaxDistance` ("distance"). At run time the session raises the same diagnostics (once each, as a script error through the listener), and its own "frame" and "refused" ones (decision 5).
- **Destroying a joint's connected entity** during play leaves the joint naming no entity: it is not created, with the "connected" diagnostic once. A game clears `ConnectedBody`, removes the joint or destroys the joint's entity first; recorded with the limits (`Docs/Backlog.md` §6 at landing).

## Risks

- **Jolt's asserts and dangling constraints.** Decision 2 refuses or clamps every value content can bring and the world destroys a body's joints before it; decision 5 does it again on each of the session's removal paths; the removal-path cases and the random-joints case run under Jolt's asserts in Debug and Release.
- **Joints across histories.** Unique priorities (decision 5) and the kept frames in the hash; the two-histories cases compare the steps after.
- **Breaking near its threshold** (decision 6): per collision step; FeatureTest's weld sits well away from its resting load.
- **Long chains with large mass ratios** stretch at Jolt's default iterations (a §2 row at landing, ADR 0052).
- **Characters on jointed bodies.** A trap door holding the Warden needs a working mass and motor torque (the skills say so, W37.2); a door lighter than a character never carries it (M34's rule).
- **The merge with M36.** Both change `PhysicsWorld.*`, `PhysicsSystem.*` and their state headers; M37's hooks are single lines in `Rebuild` (`SynchronizeJointsAfterRebuild(composition)` at its end), `CreateBody`, `RemoveBody`, `PreStep`, `PostStep`, `FlushDestroyed`, the destructor, `ConnectSignals` and `DisconnectSignals`, and two guard changes: `SynchronizeBodies` also runs when only joints changed (and rebuilds only when components changed), and `AppendStateHash`'s early return also requires no kept frames. M37 lands second and keeps M36's hunks.
