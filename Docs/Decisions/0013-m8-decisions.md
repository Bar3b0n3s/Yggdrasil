# 0013 — M8 contract decisions

- **Status:** proposed by the M8 contract task; the streams implemented against decisions 1 to 19, the integration task (the contract owner) added decisions 20 to 26 for what changed after the freeze (Roadmap rule 3), and the review of the integrated milestone added decision 27. The amendments listed at the end are for the docs owner.
- **Date:** 2026-10-08
- **Context:** The M8 contract task (Roadmap rule 3) froze the public interface of Renderer I: the PBR forward passes and their materials, lights and DFG LUT; the environment bake, its importer, its cooked payload and the skybox; the post chain (tonemap, exposure, dither, blue noise, bloom, FXAA); the debug draw list, the debug lines and the text; the M8 additions to the render snapshot, `GpuResourceCache`, `PipelineFactory`, `ProjectValidator` and `viewport.screenshot`; the CPU references and the golden scenes. It wrote every Roadmap M8 acceptance test as a skipped skeleton (C++ and Python). Writing complete headers exposed places where the Architecture and Roadmap are silent or cannot be followed literally; `AGENTS.md` ("Deviations") requires a record of each.

## Decisions

Changed: `RenderSnapshot.h` (decision 5), `SceneRenderer.h` and `ViewportCapture.h` (decision 7), `GpuResourceCache.h` (decision 7). Pass helpers go under `Renderer/Private/`, named after their stream's unit.
- **`Graphics`:** `PipelineFactory.h` gains shared binding layouts (decision 7).
- **`Asset`:** `EnvironmentData.h` gains the conventions and the cooked payload, with a new `EnvironmentData.cpp` (decision 9); `BuiltinAssets.h` gains `BuiltinAssetHandles::BlueNoiseTexture` (0x0186, decision 8).
- **`AssetPipeline`:** `Importers/EnvironmentImporter.h|.cpp` (decision 9).
- **`Scene`:** `RenderExtraction.h` documents the extraction of texts (decision 11); `DebugDrawList.h` was already the second Renderer header Scene may include (`ModuleRules.json` `SnapshotHeaders`), and snapshot headers may include each other.
- **EditorCore:** `EngineAssetGenerators.h|.cpp` (decision 8); `Project/ProjectValidator.h` reports `RenderLightLimitExceededCode` of `Renderer/RenderPrepare.h` (decision 7), as it reuses Scene's load codes.

- No premake, `Dependencies.lua` or `ModuleRules.json` change was needed: new files are picked up by the globs, and every new include edge (EditorCore to Renderer for the generator list) was already allowed.

The record calls of the M8 pass list are stream A's (decision 7).

### 3. Shader programs and the stub passes

- Every M8 program is declared in `Resources/Shaders/Shaders.json` by the contract, with its entry points and permutations, and a stub `.slang` body that compiles and passes `spirv-val` (no bindings): `Bloom` (`CSDownsampleKaris`, `CSDownsample`, `CSUpsample`; permutation `BLOOM_RGBA16` 0/1), `BrdfLut` (`CSMain`), `DebugLines` (`VSMain`, `PSMain`), `EnvironmentBake` (`CSEquirectToCube`, `CSDownsampleCube`, `CSPrefilterSpecular`, `CSProjectIrradiance`, `CSReduceIrradiance`), `Fxaa` (`CSMain`), `Skybox` (`VSMain`, `PSMain`), `Text` (`VSMain`, `PSMain`) and the test program `TonemapCurves` (`CSMain`). `Scene` and `Tonemap` exist since M7.
- Each stub pass returns the stub program's layout descriptions and creates pipelines from them, so "Shaders: LayoutsMatchReflection" covers every program of the manifest (it requires that) and `GetPipelineCount()` always equals the descriptions' count (the M7 test and the editor's logged count rely on it). The stub pipelines are never bound: `Record` records nothing.
- Each stream replaces its own `.slang` files and edits only its own program objects in `Shaders.json` (entries and permutations), so the five branches merge without conflicts; a new program goes through stream A, the file's owner. New `Common/` modules belong to the stream that writes them (`Brdf.slang` A, `Tonemapping.slang` C, `Environment.slang` B after the contract).
- Stub `.slang` files carry a "Contract stub" header comment; Lint's contract step covers C++ only, so the review of each stream checks that no stub program survives.

### 4. Target formats and the per-view chain

- `SceneTargetFormats.h` fixes `SceneDepth` D32, `SceneNormals` RG16_FLOAT, `SceneColor` RGBA16_FLOAT and `LdrColor` RGBA8_UNORM, with the two framebuffer layouts the passes create pipelines for: `GetSceneColorFramebufferInfo` (forward, skybox) and `GetOverlayFramebufferInfo` (debug lines, text: the LDR target with `SceneDepth` read-only, §8.3 passes 13 and 14).
- `SceneRenderer` keeps creating its own per-view targets (ADR 0012 decision 25): `LdrColor` and its FXAA ping-pong partner (both storage image, render target and shader resource) and the bloom chain from `BloomPass::GetChainDesc`. `RenderTargetPool` and per-pass GPU timers stay with M9's contract, which changes `SceneRenderer::Create` for the profiler anyway.
- `GetFinalTexture` returns whichever LDR target holds the last image (FXAA's output, or `LdrColor`).

### 5. The render snapshot

- `RenderSnapshot` gains `Texts` (`TextItem`), `DebugDraw` (`DebugDrawList`) and `DebugView` (`RenderDebugView`), with the snapshot's own enums `RenderTextSpace` and `RenderTextAlignment` (it cannot include Scene). M9 adds the pick table and the view flags; the debug view stays its own member instead of moving into §8.2's `RenderViewFlags`, because a screenshot sets it independently of the view's flags.
- World text has no size unit in §5.3: an em is `Size / WorldTextPixelsPerMetre` metres (100, so the default 32 is 0.32 m) before the entity's scale.

### 6. The debug draw list

- Commands of §8.10: `DebugLine`, `DebugRay`, `DebugBox` (centre, half extents, rotation), `DebugSphere`, `DebugCapsule` (the segment between the hemisphere centres), `DebugArrow`, `DebugFrustum` (eight explicit corners, because a perspective camera's far plane is at infinity, §8.3) and `DebugText` (§8.10's Text3D), as a `std::variant` with a linear RGBA colour, a duration in simulation seconds and a `DebugDepthMode {Tested, OnTop}`.
- **Durations.** `Advance(delta)` removes the commands whose remaining time is spent, then ages the rest; its owner calls it once per simulation step before the step's producers add commands, so a 0-duration command is extracted once and a timed one stays at least its duration. Who owns the persistent list (the scene, or the play session) is M11's and M13's decision; extraction leaves `RenderSnapshot::DebugDraw` empty and producers append (`Append` keeps remaining times).
- At most `MaxCommands` (65,536) commands; more are dropped and counted, so a runaway producer cannot exhaust memory with the list. The drawing side bounds the tessellation separately: `BuildDebugLineVertices` stops at `MaxDebugLineVertices` (2^20 vertices, 28 MiB) and counts the commands it leaves out (`BudgetSkippedCommands`), and `DebugRenderer` logs a warning once (decision 10). Values are not validated in the list; the renderer skips non-finite primitives.
- **M11's seam:** collider visualization emits boxes, spheres, capsules and lines (mesh colliders) into the list it extracts with the view; M11's tests check the list's contents, not pixels.

### 7. Stream A: PBR forward, materials, lights, the DFG LUT

- **Pass orchestration** (`SceneRenderer.h`'s pass list) belongs to stream A: prepare, prepass, forward opaque, skybox (B's pass), transparent, bloom, tonemap, FXAA (C's), debug lines and text (D's). The other streams test their passes on synthetic inputs and appear in full renders at integration.
- **Pipelines.** Startup: 15 mesh pipelines — prepass and forward opaque for {Opaque, Mask} × {CullBack, CullFront, CullNone} and transparent (Blend) for the three cull modes. A mirroring world matrix (negative determinant) uses CullFront with `frontCounterClockwise = true` instead of M7's mirrored pipelines, and `DrawConstants::Flags` tells the shaders to flip normals and `SV_IsFrontFace`; double-sided materials use CullNone and flip back faces' normals. With the passes: 15 + Skybox 1 + Bloom 3 + Tonemap 1 + FXAA 1 + debug lines 2 + text 2 + DFG LUT 1 = **26** (`SceneRendererPipelines::StartupPipelineCount`). Mask uses the `Scene` program's alpha-mask permutation in both the prepass and the forward pass.
- **Shared binding layouts.** NVRHI accepts a binding set only for the pipeline's own layout objects, so §8.4's "one cached BindingSet per (material, version)" needs every pipeline that binds a material to share its set-1 layout: `PipelineFactory::CreateBindingLayouts` makes the layouts once and `GraphicsPipelineSpecification::SharedBindingLayouts` passes them per set (each non-null entry checked against its set's desc; a null entry makes the pipeline create that set's layout). So the prepass, whose set 0 differs from the forward pass's, still shares set 1 with it for its Mask variants, and M9's alpha-tested shadow casters and EntityId pass can do the same with their own set 0.
- **Binding sets per view (`PassBindingCache.h`).** The passes every view of a device shares (skybox, bloom, tonemap, FXAA, debug lines, text) keep no binding sets of their own: each `Record` takes the view's `PassBindingCache`, which finds a set by its layout and exact `BindingSetDesc` and holds it (and through it its resources). Every `SceneRenderer` keeps one cache per pass with its targets, clears them in `Resize`, calls `ReleaseUnused` at the end of every `Render` (a view keeps exactly what its last render bound) and destroys them with itself. Several views (the scene view, the game view, `ViewportCapture` resized per screenshot, M10's viewports) therefore never evict each other's sets, and no pass keeps a resized or destroyed view's targets alive. M7's per-renderer tonemap binding set followed the same rule; the contract implemented the cache and moved the tonemap onto it.
- **Debug-view pipelines through `SceneRendererPipelines::EnsureDebugView`.** The set stores the `PipelineFactory` as a documented back-reference, `EnsureDebugView(view)` creates a view's nine variants (no effect for Lit or a view already created; nothing kept on failure), and `SceneRenderer::Create` and `ViewportCapture::CreateForScenes` take the set by non-const reference, since a render may extend it. A host may call `EnsureDebugView` ahead of time.
- **Binding model.** Set 0 per view per §8.4 with what M8 uses: `b0` ViewConstants, `b2` EnvironmentConstants, `t0` Lights, `t3` EnvSpecular, `t4` EnvSkybox, `t5` BRDFLut, `t7` BlueNoise, `s0` LinearClamp, `s2` AnisoWrap, `s3` PointClamp, and the 80-byte `DrawConstants` push constants (at slot `b3`, ADR 0012 decision 22). M9 adds `b1`, `t1`, `t2`, `t6` and `s1`. Set 1 per material: `b0` MaterialConstants and the five maps. `ViewConstants` may gain members at its end (stream A, e.g. the light count); its existing members are frozen because B, C and D read them. `Shared/EnvironmentConstants.h` is frozen (B's skybox reads it); its layout was checked against slangc's reflection when the contract wrote it, and stream A adds it to "Shaders: SharedStructsMatchReflection" with the first program that binds it. `LightingConstants` (M7) is A's to remove.
- **`GpuResourceCache`** gains `GetMaterial` (constants and resolved maps; White, FlatNormal or Black for empty slots; the Error material's mirror for a missing one; a new `Generation` on every build or rebuild, including a texture's new version, taken from one increasing counter of the cache so no generation is ever reused, not even after `CollectStale` released the mirror) and `GetEnvironment` (both cubes as RGBA16F cube textures through `HostImageUpload`, the SH9; null for a null handle or an environment that fails to load or upload, since environments have no placeholder asset and the renderer then lights with `FallbackColor`; the cache reports each failure itself through `AssetManager::ReportDiagnostic`, once per (handle, version), with `GetOrPlaceholder`'s codes, so every manager behaves alike). Stream A implements both; they key on `(handle, version)` like meshes and textures, and `CollectStale` covers them.
- **Stale mirrors (§8.14 items 1 and 2).** No host called `CollectStale` before M8, so replaced versions stayed mirrored; with environments (about 50 MB each), materials and font atlases that matters. Every host that renders calls `GpuResourceCache::CollectStale()` and `SceneRendererPipelines::CollectStale(assets)` once per frame after its renders were executed, and both with `releaseUnused = true` after the first frame rendered once a scene was opened, closed or swapped, play mode included (`SceneRenderer.h`, "Stale mirrors"). Stream A adds the calls to `RuntimeApp` and specifies them; stream B adds them to `EditorApp`, its owner. The acceptance test "GpuResourceCache: live counts return to baseline after unloading a scene" renders a mesh, a material with its texture, an environment and a text through a `SceneRenderer`, unloads (renderer destroyed, both collections twice) and requires the live count to return to the baseline of a first load and unload (the pass-owned vertex buffers grow once and stay).
- **The DFG LUT is stream A's** (`BrdfLut.h`), although the Roadmap lists it with the IBL deliverables: it depends only on the BRDF, is generated at device startup (not at import), is read only by the forward pass and is what the "furnace for BRDF" test exercises, so placing it with the BRDF lets stream A test IBL in its own branch. Its content is Filament's multi-scatter form `(∫ Fc·Gv, ∫ Gv)` over (NdotV, perceptual roughness), stated in the header for the CPU reference.
- **Lights.** `RenderPrepare.h`'s `CullLights`: directional lights first, then point and spot lights whose bounding spheres meet the view, by importance (`Intensity · max(Color) / max(distance, 1)²`, ties by UUID); at most `MaxVisibleLights` (256). `Culled` counts every light not shaded besides the dropped ones: point and spot lights outside the view, and lights of any type, directional included, with zero or non-finite radiance or range. **`RENDER_LIGHT_LIMIT_EXCEEDED`** (`RenderLightLimitExceededCode`, defined in `RenderPrepare.h` because the Engine renderer logs it and cannot include EditorCore): the validator cannot know every view, so it reports a scene with more than 256 enabled lights (Warning, not fixable, on the scene file); the renderer logs the same code once per renderer when a view really drops lights. Sorting: opaque by (pipeline, material, mesh, snapshot order); transparent back to front by the view depth of the submesh bounds' centre, ties by UUID, then submesh. `RENDER_NO_LIGHTING` waits for M9 with `RENDER_SPOT_SHADOW_BUDGET`.
- Tests that change with the PBR renderer (the M7 `SceneRenderer` cases that check exact Lambert values, the `LitScene` golden) are stream A's and E's to update; the M7 cases set Linear, bloom off and FXAA off where they check exact values.

### 8. Stream C: tonemap, bloom, FXAA, blue noise

- **Tonemap order:** bloom lerp in linear radiance, exposure (`ViewConstants::Exposure`, which the renderer sets to `2^ExposureEV`, or 1 for debug views), the tonemapper, the sRGB OETF (unless `EncodeSrgb` is off), blue-noise triangular dither of ±1 LSB tiled by pixel coordinate (no temporal variation). One pipeline: the tonemapper and the flags are constants, not permutations.
- **The tonemapper formulas are frozen in `TonemapPass.h`**, because stream C's shader and stream E's CPU reference implement them independently and the curve test compares them within 1e-3, far less than the variants differ by: AgX's inset and outset matrices, its log2 range [-12.47393, 4.026069], the 6th-order sigmoid polynomial and the 2.2 power to display-linear; ACES as Hill's input matrix, RRT+ODT fit and output matrix without an exposure pre-scale; Khronos PBR Neutral's reference constants; Linear's clamp. Architecture §8.9 names the tonemappers only.
- **Bloom:** three pipelines for the device's format (`BLOOM_RGBA16` permutation for the RGBA16_FLOAT fallback, §8.1), the per-view chain from `GetChainDesc` (half resolution, at most six mips), no threshold. The upsample adds each level into the next larger one and the last step divides by the mip count, so mip 0 is the average of the chain's levels and a uniform scene blooms to itself (a GPU test checks it on RGBA16_FLOAT; the two-format test creates R11G11B10_FLOAT only where the device supports it as a storage image). `Record` returns the chain, or null when it recorded nothing, so a caller never composites an unwritten chain.
- **FXAA:** FXAA 3.11 quality preset 12, luma from the encoded colour, between the two LDR targets. `Record` returns the texture holding the result.
- **Blue noise:** a seeded void-and-cluster generator using Core/DetMath for the Gaussian, so its committed XXH64 is identical on every compiler and configuration; the 64² R8 built-in `engine://Textures/BlueNoise` (0x0186), a Generated entry of `EngineAssets.json` that stream C adds with the generator (adding it earlier would make every export fail on a generator that returns Unsupported). The renderer asks the cache for the texture only when `AssetManager::GetAssetType(BlueNoiseTexture)` is `Texture` (a check that records no diagnostic), and passes no blue noise, which turns the dither off, when the check fails or the mirror is a placeholder; going through `GetOrPlaceholder` blindly would log `ASSET_MISSING` at Error in every test whose manager lacks the built-in.
- **Tonemapper curves:** the GPU test evaluates the shader functions through the `TonemapCurves` test program (`Tests/Source/Support/TonemapCurvesProgram.h`, like M5's `MatrixConventionProgram`) in 32-bit float, so the 1,024-point comparison is not limited by 8-bit quantization.

### 9. Stream B: environments, the importer, the baker, the skybox

- **Conventions** (`EnvironmentData.h`, shared with the shaders and the CPU references): right-handed, +Y up; the equirectangular centre is −Z and u = 0.75 is +X; cube faces and texel directions follow Vulkan's face selection; `IrradianceSH9` holds the coefficients of irradiance / π (a constant environment evaluates to its radiance), Hanning-windowed with w = 4 (`EnvironmentData::IrradianceShWindow`); rotation turns the environment by +Rotation about +Y (`RotateEnvironmentDirection`).
- **Cooked payload** FormatVersion 1: four `uint32` sizes, the nine SH coefficients, the skybox texels, the specular texels; sizes checked before allocation. The `Environment` loader joins `RegisterBuiltinLoaders` with stream B's implementation (registering it earlier would break the M6 tests that use Environment as the example of a type without a loader); stream B updates those tests.
- **`EnvironmentImporter`** (id `Environment`, version 1, settings `EnvironmentImportSettings {ClampLuminance, MaxLuminance}`): claims `.hdr` and, only to refuse it precisely, `.exr`; runs on the main thread; Unsupported with the GPU hint without a baker. Stream B registers it and its settings type (`RegisterBuiltinImporters`, `RegisterAssetPipelineTypes`) and updates the M6 tests that list the importers. The sun-heavy `Sky` built-in turns `ClampLuminance` on in `EngineAssets.json`.
- **Reuse without a GPU (§7.4, "keyed as in §7.5"):** before calling an importer that needs a GPU it does not have, the `EditorAssetManager` serves the import from any cooked artifact with the same cache key — under the asset's own handle (the normal cache hit), under any other handle of the project cache, then in the engine cooked cache. So a copy of an already baked HDRI, or of a built-in HDRI the engine cache holds, loads under `--renderer none`; anything else reports `ASSET_IMPORT_FAILED` with the hint. Stream B implements this in `EditorAssetManager` and `AssetCache`; `IAssetImporter.h` is unchanged.
- **`EnvironmentBaker`:** §8.6 steps 2 to 4, `BakeWithOptions` for the oracle tests' small cubes, five pipelines created with the editor's other pipelines; the skybox face is `max(1, min(1024, width / 4))` or an explicit power of two up to 1024 (what `ValidateEnvironmentData` accepts); specular mip 0 is the skybox resampled to the specular size from the smallest level at least that size, or from level 0 when the skybox is smaller (the CPU reference states the same rule); injected by `EditorApp` into `EditorContextSpecification::EnvironmentBaker` and the `--bake-engine-assets` run when a device exists. CI.py's bake stage then runs with a renderer (with `--gpu-optional` semantics for hosts without a device: the environments are skipped with a warning there, as today).
- **Exports:** Engine.pak takes the built-in environments from the engine cooked cache like any other File entry (baking them first when the editor has a baker). Without a GPU and without a cached bake, the environments are left out of Engine.pak with a warning, as in M7 but with the GPU hint instead of "until M8", and the export fails only when an included asset (a scene's `EnvironmentComponent`, typically) references a left-out environment: that reference is a `CheckReferences` issue with the hint `start the editor with a GPU once to bake this environment` (§7.5: "as long as the bakes exist"). Failing every bakeless export instead would break every automation test that exports from the harness's `--renderer none` editors and their per-test engine caches (`test_export_tiny_project`, `test_export_is_deterministic`, the smoke tests, `test_exported_*`, `test_runtime_automation_subset`, the game-view export) and every export on a machine without a device; the tiny game references no environment. Stream B keeps those M7 Python tests green unchanged and updates `Exporter.h`'s description of the engine entries.
- **`SkyboxPass`:** a fullscreen triangle at depth 0, depth test GreaterOrEqual against `SceneDepth` without writes, the cube along each pixel's view ray (constant for orthographic cameras, §8.3) at `SkyboxLod`, times `Intensity`.

### 10. Stream D: debug lines

`DebugRenderer`: two pipelines (depth-tested and on top) for the overlay framebuffer, LineList from one geometrically growing vertex buffer owned by the renderer (any number of views record one after another; NVRHI orders each record's write after earlier reads), at most `MaxDebugLineVertices` per record. `BuildDebugLineVertices` is the pure tessellation, with the segment counts its header documents and the vertex budget (a command that does not fit is left out whole and counted), so the CPU tests need no GPU. Linear colours are encoded in the shader.

### 11. Stream D: text

- `TextLayout.h`: `LayoutText` (em units, top-left origin, baseline at Ascent, line height Ascent − Descent + LineGap, kerning, alignment; skipped codepoints counted) and `PlaceScreenText` (Anchor · viewport + Offset · scale − Pivot · block, scale = height / 1080).
- `TextRenderer`: two pipelines; world texts depth-tested, then screen texts and the debug list's labels. **Font atlases are mirrored by the renderer itself**, a pass-owned GPU object as §8.14 item 1 allows, keyed by (handle, version) and released by `CollectStale` (forwarded by `SceneRendererPipelines::CollectStale`), so stream D needs nothing from `GpuResourceCache`.
- Extraction (`Scene/RenderExtraction.cpp`, stream D): every effectively enabled `TextComponent` with non-empty text, mapped field by field, at the rendered world matrix.

### 12. Debug views and viewport.screenshot

- The six views of M8 are `RenderDebugView` {Lit, Albedo, Normals, Roughness, Metallic, Emissive}; M9 appends AO, ShadowCascades and Overdraw. The enumerator's value is the specialization constant's value.
- **`viewport.screenshot {debugView}`** keeps its M5 wire type (a string): names parse ignoring ASCII case, absent or empty is Lit, M9's names are Unsupported at `/debugView` naming M9, anything else is InvalidParams at `/debugView` listing the valid names; the handler sets `RenderSnapshot::DebugView`. Stream A implements it in `Engine/Automation/Methods/ScreenshotMethods.cpp`, updates the param's description and regenerates `Tools/MCP/catalog.json`.
- **Post chain of a debug view:** no skybox, bloom, FXAA or dither, exposure 1, Linear, the OETF only for Albedo and Emissive, so a data view stores v as round(255 v) and tests read exact values.
- **Lazy debug-view pipelines (deviation from §8.12).** A non-Lit view's nine forward variants are created the first time a snapshot asks for it, through `SceneRendererPipelines::EnsureDebugView` (decision 7), and kept (`DebugViewPipelineCount`). Creating all of them at startup would be 71 pipelines instead of 26 on every device, every test fixture and every exported game, for a debugging path; ImGui's per-format pipelines are the precedent (ADR 0009 decision 25). A creation failure there is `FatalError(OutOfMemory)` like any pipeline failure (§8.14 item 7). The count test checks both numbers.

### 13. Stub behaviour

Stubs start with `ENGINE_CONTRACT_STUB();` and fail fast or return neutral values, so stream A can call every pass before the others land: the passes create their stub pipelines and record nothing; `BloomPass::Record` returns null, `FxaaPass::Record` the source; `BrdfLut::GetTexture` is null; `EnvironmentBaker::Bake` and the importer are Unsupported with the GPU hint; `GpuResourceCache::GetMaterial` returns an empty mirror and `GetEnvironment` null; `SceneRendererPipelines::EnsureDebugView` is Unsupported; the environment codec, `BlueNoise`, `RenderPrepare`, `TextLayout` and the CPU references return empty values or Unsupported. Nothing a stub does can hang, open a device, spawn a process or write outside a temporary directory.

### 14. Test support and the golden scenes

- **`RenderReference.h`** (stream E): the CPU references the oracle tests compare with — Hammersley, GGX, Smith, Schlick, GGX importance sampling, the DFG terms and LUT, the compensated specular albedo (BRDF furnace), cube texel directions and solid angles, the equirectangular mapping and resampling, the brute-force GGX prefilter (16²), the SH9 projection and evaluation, the four tonemappers, the OETF, the 1,024 tonemapper sample points and binary16 conversions — written from the papers and the frozen headers, never from the shaders.
- **`GoldenScene.h`** (stream E): `RenderGoldenScene` renders `Projects/FeatureTest/Assets/Scenes/Golden/<Name>.scene` from a temporary copy of the project through an `EditorAssetManager` with the engine resources, an `EnvironmentBaker` and the editor's generators, and `ViewportCapture`, at 640×360 with FXAA on; `ComposeImageGrid` builds the Tonemappers golden. The golden scenes are produced by a committed scaffold, `Projects/FeatureTest/Scaffold/Golden.jsonl` run with `Editor --batch` (§15.5), and the DebugDraw golden appends its primitives to the extracted snapshot until scripts emit them (M13). The images are committed for this device class after the integration, with `LitScene` regenerated.
- `InMemoryAssetManager` serves exact assets (a constant environment for the furnace, materials with given factors); `AssetTestFixtureOptions` mounts the engine resources (Default font, Studio and Sky) with an in-memory engine cache.

### 20. Frozen headers: reviewed additions and changes

- **`TextRenderer.h`:** `TextRenderInputs` gains `std::optional<glm::mat4> CameraView` at its end (stream D's proposal), which the `SceneRenderer` fills with the snapshot camera's view matrix. With it, `Record` neither records nor counts a label on or behind the camera plane (view-space z >= 0, the test the vertex shader applies), so `SceneRenderStats::TextDraws` counts what can be seen; without it every label is recorded and counted and the shader drops the hidden ones (the image is the same, which the test checks). Atlases are uploaded through `HostImageUpload` as the contract said: stream D had used a command list of the renderer's own because `HostImageUpload` opened an immediate list, which NVRHI refuses while the caller's frame list is open; once `HostImageUpload`'s lists stopped being immediate (decision 22) the reason was gone, and the atlas takes the host-image-copy path where the device allows it.
- **`SceneTargetFormats.h`, `SkyboxPass.h`, `DebugRenderer.h`, `TextRenderer.h`:** "SceneDepth bound read-only" now reads "tested against SceneDepth without writing it". The overlay framebuffer attaches `SceneDepth` as an ordinary depth attachment and its pipelines disable depth writes: NVRHI stores a read-only depth attachment with `VK_ATTACHMENT_STORE_OP_STORE`, which synchronization validation counts as a depth write that the read-only state's barriers do not cover, so the next frame's layout transition of `SceneDepth` was reported as a write-after-write hazard once the text pass ran in a full render. Keeping `SceneDepth` in `DepthWrite` from the prepass to the overlays needs no transition at all. M9's GTAO, which samples `SceneDepth`, decides again if a pass needs it bound as a shader resource during the overlays.
- **`Shared/ViewConstants.h`** gains `LightCount` and three padding words at its end (400 bytes), as decision 7 allowed; **`Shared/DrawConstants.h`** gains the `DrawFlagMirrored` bit and the debug-view specialization constants (`SceneDebugViewConstantId`, one value per `RenderDebugView`); **`Shared/MaterialConstants.h`** holds the §8.5 factors and `Flags` with `MaterialFlagEmissiveMap`. ### 21. Choices the streams made where the contract was silent (accepted)

- **A:**
  - Mask materials drop fragments by writing `SV_Coverage = 0` instead of `discard`: Slang compiles `discard` to `OpDemoteToHelperInvocation` for SPIR-V 1.6, which needs `shaderDemoteToHelperInvocation`, a Vulkan 1.3 feature `GraphicsDevice` does not enable. Enabling it would allow a plain `discard` later.
  - `MaterialFlagEmissiveMap`: an empty emissive slot binds Black (§8.4), while glTF says the emissive factor emits on its own; the flag tells the shader whether to multiply by the map.
  - Artist-unit lights: a light of radiance C·I delivers π·C·I illuminance to a surface facing it, so a white Lambertian surface lit head-on by a directional light of intensity 1 has radiance 1 (the walking skeleton's Lambert convention, kept).
  - The BRDF and lighting modules `Common/Brdf.slang` and `Common/Lighting.slang`: geometric specular anti-aliasing (Tokuyoshi and Kaplanyan, with Filament's variance and threshold), Jimenez's multi-bounce diffuse occlusion, Lagarde's specular occlusion and horizon occlusion for normal maps, and the 0.045 roughness clamp.
  - Material binding sets are cached per view by (handle, generation) and released at the end of a render that did not use them, like the shared passes' `PassBindingCache`; a material that leaves and re-enters the view gets a new set.
  - The skeleton of "HotReload: texture change re-uploads" expected one mirrored texture after the collection; with the frozen design it is four (the new version plus the White, FlatNormal and Black slot defaults `GetMaterial` resolves through `GetTexture`), and the material file references its texture by handle (the importer rejects a path).
- **B:**
  - Equirectangular sources are limited to 8,192 texels wide (`ImportFailed` in the importer, `InvalidArgument` in the baker), which keeps the RGBA32F upload at most 512 MiB; a 1,024 skybox face, the largest, needs no more than a 4k source.
  - Baked texels are clamped to 65,504, the largest finite binary16 value (an infinity would fail `ValidateEnvironmentData`).
  - SH9 is projected from the first skybox level of at most 64², with exact texel solid angles and a fixed-order reduction; the CPU then normalizes, applies the cosine lobe over π and the Hanning window.
  - Specular mips beyond 0 use GGX importance sampling with filtered importance sampling (Křivánek and Colbert) and sample counts rising linearly with roughness; the bake's per-dispatch values are push constants (`Shared/EnvironmentBakeConstants.h`).
  - The editor notices a scene change for the stale-mirror collections from the shown edit scene and play session plus the `SceneOpened` and `PlayStateChanged` events (a dropped event counts as a change).
  - CI.py's bake stage runs `Editor --renderer vulkan`; without a device it fails, and with `--gpu-optional` it reruns with `--renderer none` and ends as a warning. `.github/workflows/ci.yml`'s comments say so (the integration updated them: Linux bakes on Lavapipe, the Windows and macOS runners leave the environments out).
- **C:**
  - The tonemap replaces NaN radiance with 0 and clamps exposed radiance to 65,504 before the tonemapper; the dither fades out within the first and last 8-bit level, so exact black and white stay exact; bloom's first downsample replaces NaN with 0 and clamps samples to 65,024 / 6 (`MaxMipCount`), so the chain's running sums stay finite in both storage formats.
  - The post passes' push constants are `Shared/TonemapConstants.h` (Tonemap and TonemapCurves) and `Shared/BloomConstants.h`. The Tonemap layout is `b0` View, push constants at slot 1, `t0` SceneColor, `t1` Bloom (mip 0), `t7` BlueNoise, `s0` LinearClamp, `u0` LdrColor; a missing bloom or blue-noise input is bound to SceneColor and never read, so no placeholder texture exists.
  - AgX's white point after the 2.2 power is 0.9965, which still encodes to 255.
  - The contract's "EncodeSrgb off" test stored 0.5, an exact rounding tie (127.5) that this NVIDIA device rounds to 127; the test stores 0.75 (191).
  - The committed blue-noise XXH64 is `0x5F7E9B5C0BE7B81C`, identical in Debug and Release.
- **D:**
  - UTF-8 decoding follows the Unicode well-formed sequences; each ill-formed sequence counts as one skipped codepoint, kerning applies across skipped codepoints and resets at each line feed, and `\r` is ignored.
  - A null font and a missing font share the Default font's atlas; a failed atlas upload is reported once per font version.
  - The text vertex buffer grows with the texts, up to a budget of 2^20 vertices per record like the debug lines' `MaxDebugLineVertices` (the review added the budget, decision 27), and a buffer that cannot be created is the record's `Gpu` error with the texts skipped.
- **E:**
  - `RenderReference.cpp` records the DFG estimator's exact sample mapping, which the GPU generator shares (`BrdfLut` matches it within 2e-3); the compensated specular albedo integrates the single-scatter part by direct quadrature, so the BRDF furnace checks the BRDF terms against the LUT instead of an algebraic identity.
  - `asset.import` reads project or absolute paths only, so the fixtures the scaffold imports sit in `Projects/FeatureTest/Scaffold/Sources` as byte-identical copies of `Tests/Data/Assets`; "GoldenScene: the scaffold's sources and their imported copies are the Tests/Data fixtures" fails if they drift.
  - Every golden scene turns SSAO off and disables shadow casting, so M9's shadows and GTAO leave these images unchanged; AlphaModes' cut-out uses the alpha gradient of `Rgba.png` (no alpha-checker fixture exists); `RenderGoldenScene` fails on any load or scan error and any Error-level asset diagnostic instead of returning an image with placeholders.

### 27. Review fixes

The review of the integrated milestone found these; each fix has its regression test.

- **Stale-mirror scheduling is shared and tested** (decision 7). `Renderer/StaleMirrorSchedule.h` (`NoteSceneChange`, `NoteRendered`, `TakeReleaseUnused`) decides when the hosts' collections pass `releaseUnused`: exactly once, at the first collection after a frame rendered the newly shown scene. `EditorCore/ShownSceneTracker.h` is the editor's notice of a change: the edit scene and the play session's scene compared with the previous frame's (typed pointers, never dereferenced), plus the `SceneOpened` and `PlayStateChanged` events since, a dropped event counting too. `EditorApp` and `RuntimeApp` (which notes its start scene once) only call them. The "StaleMirrorSchedule" and "ShownSceneTracker" cases test opening, closing and swapping scenes, play start and stop, a replacement seen only through its event, dropped events, no release before a rendered frame and exactly one after it. An automation method reporting `GpuResourceCache::GetStats` for an end-to-end Python test needs a `RegisterMethods.cpp` change, which is the lead's (requested).
- **Environment intensity is bounded.** `MakeEnvironmentConstants` (`Renderer/Private/LightingInputs.h`) clamps it to 65,504 (`EnvironmentBakeMaxHalf`, the bound of the texels and SH it scales) in both branches and zeroes a scaled SH9 coefficient that is not finite, so no infinity reaches the forward shader, whose SH sum and diffuse products would turn it into NaN and the tonemap into black. `EnvironmentComponent.Intensity` gets `Max = 65504`.
- **Text never uploads a non-finite vertex and has a budget** (decision 21, D). An item with a glyph vertex beyond float's range (finite but huge sizes, offsets or matrices) is skipped and not counted; at most 2^20 vertices (48 MiB) per record, an item that does not fit left out whole and a warning logged once per renderer (`TextRenderer.h`'s description; no signature change). `TextComponent.Size` gets `Max = 100000` (pixels per em at 1080p: about 93 screen heights, or 1 km per em in the world).
- **Debug lines likewise:** `BuildDebugLineVertices` skips a command, counted in `SkippedCommands`, whose tessellated vertices overflow (`DebugRenderer.h`'s description).
- **Invariant positions.** The forward passes test against the prepass depth without writing, which needs bit-identical clip positions across the pipelines. Vulkan guarantees that only for a position decorated `Invariant`, which Slang 2026.8 cannot emit (no invariant modifier; `precise` emits no `NoContraction` either), so `Scene.slang`'s `MultiplyExact` emits the vertex shader's two products as `OpVectorTimesMatrix` decorated `NoContraction` in SPIR-V assembly: no driver may fuse their operations differently per pipeline. "SceneRenderer: the forward pass leaves no hole where the prepass covered a transformed sphere" guards it on every device class CI runs, Lavapipe included.
- **`ShaderLight` in the reflection check:** the parser collects a structured buffer's element struct (`resultType`), sized by the end of its last field since slangc reports no stride (`ShaderReflection.h`), and "Shaders: SharedStructsMatchReflection" lists `ShaderLight`.
- **The BRDF on the GPU against the CPU references.** The white furnace cannot fail on the LUT's content (f0 = 1 cancels DFG2; a white dielectric's diffuse makes up for its specular). "SceneRenderer: grey surfaces under a constant environment reflect the DFG LUT's compensated albedo" (f0 0.5 and 0.04 on LUT rows) and "SceneRenderer: a directional light shades head-on as the CPU BRDF does" (D, V, F and the compensation) compare with `RenderReference.h` within 2 LSB.
- **One count of debug views:** `RenderDebugViewCount` in `RenderSnapshot.h` (the last enumerator plus one) sizes the view names (checked complete at compile time), the per-view pipelines and `viewport.screenshot`'s list of names, so M9's views are one compile-checked edit. `SceneRendererPipelines::EnsureDebugView` documents its `InvalidArgument`.
