# Animation

A reference page of the `game-building` skill (`skills/game-building`), for its steps "3. Scenes, assets and prefabs" and "4. Scripts": skinned and morphed characters imported from glTF, their Animators, the editor's animation preview, and what a builder checks. Scripts that play, blend and listen to clips are `skills/luau-gameplay/animation`.

- **Import.** `asset_import {source: "<file>.gltf", destDir}` imports a glTF's skins, animations and morph targets with its meshes: each skin becomes a `Skeleton` sub-asset (`skin:<i>[:<name>]`), each animation of joints or morph weights an `AnimationClip` (`animation:<i>[:<name>]`), and a skinned mesh keeps its joint weights (at most four a vertex) and morph targets. `engine_call {method: "asset.info", params: {asset}}` lists the sub-assets and their keys; an asset reference takes `"Assets/Models/Hero.gltf#animation:0:Walk"`. Rigid animation of nodes that are not joints, cameras and lights are skipped with `ASSET_CONTENT_SKIPPED`, and so is a clip shorter than 1 ms.
- **Events.** glTF has no animation events: list them in the import settings, `asset_import {..., settings: {AnimationEvents: [{Animation: "animation:0:Walk", Name: "Footstep", Time: 0.25}]}}` or later `engine_call {method: "asset.setImportSettings"}`. An event naming no clip of the file, or past its clip's end, fails the import naming it. Scripts receive them in `OnAnimationEvent`.
- **The prefab.** `prefab_instantiate` of the glTF builds the hierarchy without entities for the joints: an `Animator` (its `Skeleton` and one layer playing the first clip that animates it) on the skin's common ancestor that is not a joint, and a `SkinnedMesh` beside each skinned or morphed `MeshRenderer`; `entity_get` shows which entity holds the Animator. A mesh drawn by a SkinnedMesh follows the nearest Animator on its entity or above it.
- **The Animator** (`component_schema {name: "Animator"}`): `Layers`, at most eight `{Clip, Time, Speed, Weight, Loop}` blended in order over the rest pose, each only on the joints and morph targets its clip keys; `Playing` (the times advance in the fixed step); `RootMotion` `None` (the hips walk away from the entity), `Apply` (the entity's Transform moves) or `Extract` (reported to scripts only). Its fields are scene data: they are saved, hashed and replayed, so set the starting layers before recording replays. There is no state machine: scripts set layers and tween weights.
- **Morph targets.** `SkinnedMesh.MorphWeights` holds one weight in [-1, 1] per target in the mesh's order (a missing one is the target's default); a clip's weight channel overrides the weights it keys.
- **Preview in the editor.** Edit scenes draw skinned meshes in their bind pose. `animation_preview {entity, time?, playing?}` poses one Animator of an open edit scene at its layers' times plus `time` seconds (0 to 10^6), without an undo step, a revision or a file change, so `viewport_screenshot` and `entity_bounds` of the edit scene show the pose; `playing: true` advances it with the editor's frames, and `{entity, stop: true}` ends it. One preview runs at a time and belongs to its scene: previewing another Animator or scene, switching the active tab or closing the scene ends it. Play poses every Animator itself, so the preview refuses the play scene (`play_stop` first).
- **Checks.** `project_validate` warns `ANIMATION_NO_ANIMATOR` (a skinned mesh with no Animator above it) and `ANIMATION_SKELETON_MISMATCH` (an Animator whose Skeleton has fewer joints than the mesh's skin); both draw the bind pose. A suite pins a pose with `Test.GetPoseDigest(entity)`, the same in the Editor, Release and Dist runs, and a replay that plays clips from input proves the animation deterministic (`skills/game-building/replays`).
- **Limits.** 256 joints a skeleton, 32 morph targets a mesh, eight layers an Animator. Poses are sampled in the fixed step and not interpolated between ticks, so animation looks stepped at the tick rate. Scene raycasts test a skinned mesh's bind-pose triangles; picking in the viewport sees the drawn pose.
